# Tableau Desktop Interface: Data Pane, Shelves & Canvas Tour

*Reading Time: 8 Mins* | *Level: Beginner to Advanced*

---

## 1. The Anatomy of a Tableau Worksheet
The Tableau Desktop authoring window is structured into distinct functional zones:
- **Data Pane (Left):** Displays tables, Dimensions (Blue), and Measures (Green).
- **Columns Shelf (Top):** Fields placed here create vertical columns (X-axis or column headers).
- **Rows Shelf (Top):** Fields placed here create horizontal rows (Y-axis or row headers).
- **Marks Card:** The heart of VizQL styling:
  - Color, Size, Label, Detail, Tooltip, Shape.
- **Filters & Pages Shelves:** Slicing and page-by-page animation.
- **View / Canvas:** Where charts and tables are drawn.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. In Tableau Desktop, what happens when you drag a Measure onto the 'Rows' shelf and a Dimension onto the 'Columns' shelf?
A. Tableau draws a vertical bar chart
B. Tableau creates a map
C. Tableau creates a text table
D. The software closes
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Rows (Y-axis numerical measure) and Columns (X-axis discrete categories) generate a standard vertical bar chart.
---

### 2. Which shelf on the Marks Card allows you to add text values directly on top of visual marks?
A. Label (or Text)
B. Tooltip
C. Detail
D. Color
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Dropping a field onto 'Label' displays its values as data labels on the chart.
---

### 3. What does the 'Show Me' menu (top-right corner) do in Tableau?
A. Recommends suitable chart types based on the combination of Dimensions and Measures currently selected
B. Shows user account details
C. Shows hidden files
D. Opens the help documentation
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** 'Show Me' assesses selected fields and highlights valid visual layouts (e.g., heatmaps, scatterplots, treemaps).
---

### 4. What does dropping a field onto the 'Detail' mark card shelf accomplish?
A. Splits the visualization into a finer level of granularity without changing color or size
B. Adds a border
C. Deletes details
D. Enlarges fonts
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** 'Detail' alters the visualization's Level of Detail (LoD) by adding categorical granularity without assigning visual encodings.
---

### 5. What is the purpose of the 'Pages' shelf in Tableau?
A. Flips through a sequence of pages automatically (creating animated time-series replays)
B. Prints paper pages
C. Creates new tabs
D. Navigates bookmarks
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The Pages shelf creates interactive flipbooks or animated timelines (e.g. tracking sales month-by-month).
