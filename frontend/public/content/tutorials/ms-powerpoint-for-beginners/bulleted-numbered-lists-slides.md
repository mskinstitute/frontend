# Bulleted & Numbered Lists, Indentation, Line Spacing

Bulleted lists are ubiquitous in presentations, but poorly spaced walls of bullet points are a leading cause of audience disengagement ("Death by PowerPoint"). Mastering bullet styling, indentation levels, line spacing, and modern visual conversions transforms mundane lists into compelling points.

---

## Creating & Customizing Bulleted Lists

Selecting any body text placeholder activates list controls on the **Home** tab:
- **Bulleted List Drop-down**: Click the arrow next to the **Bullets** button to choose from standard shapes (Solid Circles, Hollow Circles, Square Bullets, Star Bullets, or Checkmarks).
- **Custom Bullets & Numbering**: Click **Bullets and Numbering** at the bottom of the gallery:
  - **Color**: Give bullets an accent color (e.g., brand orange or green checkmarks) distinct from the text.
  - **Size**: Scale bullets relative to text size (default is 100%, 75%–80% often looks more refined).
  - **Picture Bullets**: Use your company's mini vector logo as bullet icons.
  - **Character Bullets**: Pick any Unicode symbol or emoji.

---

## Indentation Levels (List Hierarchy)

Hierarchical lists distinguish primary points from supporting details:
- **Demote (Increase Indent Level)**: Press **Tab** (or click **Increase List Level** on the Home tab). The text moves right, font size decreases slightly, and the bullet style shifts to secondary.
- **Promote (Decrease Indent Level)**: Press **Shift + Tab** (or click **Decrease List Level**). The text shifts left back to parent status.

---

## Perfecting Line and Paragraph Spacing

Cramped text looks amateurish. Modifying spacing breathes clarity into slides:
1. Select the text placeholder.
2. Click the **Line Spacing** button on the **Home** tab:
   - Select **1.2** or **1.5 lines** to prevent lines within a single bullet from crowding each other.
3. Click **Line Spacing Options** to adjust paragraph spacing:
   - **Before / After Spacing**: Set **After: 6 pt to 12 pt**. This automatically creates clean separation between distinct bullet items without having to press the Enter key twice!

---

## The Rule of 6x6 & The SmartArt Alternative

### The 6x6 Guideline
To maintain audience focus:
- Maximum **6 bullet points** per slide.
- Maximum **6 words** per bullet point.
- Slides are visual aids for the audience, not a teleprompter script for the speaker.

### Converting Bullets to SmartArt in One Click
When text lists feel dull, convert them into dynamic infographics instantly:
1. Click anywhere inside your bulleted list.
2. On the **Home** tab, click **Convert to SmartArt Graphic**.
3. Hover over the layout options to preview your points as:
   - Horizontal Process Chevrons
   - Vertical Block Lists
   - Icon Card Grids
   - Cycle Diagrams
4. Click to apply. PowerPoint converts the raw text into editable vector shapes with automated layout management!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which keyboard shortcut increases the indent level (demoting a bullet point to a secondary sub-bullet)?
A. Tab
B. Shift + Tab
C. Ctrl + Tab
D. Alt + Tab
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Pressing Tab at the start of a bulleted line increases the indent level, creating a subordinate sub-bullet. Pressing Shift + Tab promotes it back.

---

### 2. How can you add consistent breathing room between distinct bullet points without pressing Enter multiple times?
A. Add blank spaces with the spacebar
B. Set the "After" paragraph spacing to 6 pt - 12 pt in Line Spacing Options
C. Increase slide dimensions
D. Underline the text
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Configuring Paragraph Spacing "After" (e.g., 6 pt to 12 pt) adds consistent spacing between list items automatically without creating empty lines.

---

### 3. What does the "Convert to SmartArt Graphic" button on the Home tab do?
A. Replaces all words with clip art illustrations
B. Transforms a bulleted text list into a structured graphic diagram like process chevrons or cards
C. Sends the presentation to Microsoft Paint
D. Translates the text into another language
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Convert to SmartArt Graphic analyzes bulleted hierarchy and converts the plain text into diagrams, flowcharts, or cards.

---

### 4. What is the classic presentation design guideline known as the "6x6 Rule"?
A. 6 images and 6 tables per presentation
B. A maximum of 6 bullet points per slide, and a maximum of 6 words per bullet point
C. 6 slides delivered in 6 minutes
D. 6 slides per printed page
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The 6x6 rule advises presenters to limit slides to no more than 6 bullet points, each containing roughly 6 words or fewer, to avoid overwhelming audiences.

---

### 5. Which shortcut promotes a sub-bullet point back to a primary top-level bullet point?
A. Tab
B. Shift + Tab
C. Ctrl + Shift + P
D. Alt + Left Arrow
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Shift + Tab decreases the list indent level, promoting a child sub-bullet back up to parent bullet status.
