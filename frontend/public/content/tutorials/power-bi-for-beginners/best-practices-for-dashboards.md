# Best Practices for Dashboards & UI Design

*Reading Time: 8 Mins* | *Level: Beginner to Advanced*

---

## 1. Golden Rules of Power BI Dashboard Design
1. **The 5-Second Rule:** A stakeholder should grasp the core operational health of the business within 5 seconds of opening the page.
2. **Visual Hierarchy (Z-Pattern / F-Pattern):**
   - **Top:** High-level KPI summary cards.
   - **Middle:** Trend line charts and categorical breakdowns.
   - **Bottom:** Granular detail tables or matrix drill-downs.
3. **Limit Visual Count:** Never put more than 6 to 8 visual elements on a single page. If you need more, create another page!
4. **Descriptive Titles:** Instead of "Sales Chart", write "Monthly Net Revenue Trend (FY 2026)".

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is the '5-Second Rule' in business dashboard design?
A. Decision-makers should be able to comprehend the company's overall performance status within 5 seconds of glancing at the dashboard
B. The report must load in 5 seconds
C. The user must close the report after 5 seconds
D. Data updates every 5 seconds
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Dashboards must deliver immediate clarity without requiring manual calculation or searching.
---

### 2. What is the recommended maximum number of visuals on a single Power BI report page?
A. 6 to 8 focused visuals
B. Exactly 25 visuals
C. 50 visuals
D. As many as can fit
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Overloading a page beyond 6-8 visuals causes cognitive fatigue and degrades canvas rendering performance.
---

### 3. How should visual elements be arranged based on natural reading flow (Z-pattern)?
A. Executive KPIs at top-left, contextual charts in the center, and detailed tabular data at the bottom
B. Random placement
C. All tables at the top, small numbers at the bottom
D. Everything in the center
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Natural western reading flow moves left-to-right, top-to-bottom, making the upper-left quadrant prime real estate for top metrics.
---

### 4. What feature provides contextual helper text when a user hovers their mouse over a data point on a chart?
A. Tooltips (Report Page Tooltips)
B. Slicers
C. Bookmarks
D. Push notifications
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Tooltips display dynamic detail cards on hover without cluttering the main canvas.
---

### 5. Why are Bookmarks and Buttons used together in Power BI?
A. To create interactive storytelling experiences, such as toggling between chart views or resetting all filters with one click
B. To save web pages
C. To print documents
D. To change Windows passwords
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Bookmarks capture canvas states, enabling interactive button toggles (e.g. Table View vs Chart View).
