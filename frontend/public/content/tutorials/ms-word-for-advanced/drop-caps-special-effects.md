# Drop Caps & Special Effects

## Editorial Typography: Drop Caps & Pull Quotes

In professional magazine, newsletter, and book publishing, an ornate, oversized initial capital letter at the beginning of a chapter draws reader attention into the narrative. In typography, this is called a **Drop Cap**.

### 1. Inserting a Drop Cap

1. Click anywhere inside the opening paragraph of your chapter or article.
2. Navigate to **Insert** tab > **Text** group > click **Drop Cap**.
3. Choose from two core visual layouts:
   - **Dropped**: The oversized letter drops down inside the paragraph, and body lines wrap snugly around its right side.
   - **In Margin**: The oversized letter is pushed out into the left blank margin, leaving the body text starting flush at the normal margin line.

### 2. Customizing Drop Cap Options

Click **Insert > Drop Cap > Drop Cap Options...**:
- **Font**: You can assign an elegant decorative serif or gothic font (e.g. *Old English Text MT*, *Garamond*, or *Georgia*) to the Drop Cap while the rest of the paragraph remains in clean modern Calibri!
- **Lines to drop**: Controls how many vertical lines tall the initial letter becomes (default is **3 lines**; can be set from 1 to 10 lines).
- **Distance from text**: Configures horizontal breathing room between the drop cap letter and the following body text (e.g. `0.1 in` prevents letters like 'W' from colliding with text).

### 3. Styling Editorial Pull Quotes

A **Pull Quote** is a memorable sentence excerpted from the article and enlarged in a decorative box to grab scanning readers:
1. Go to **Insert > Text Box > Built-in Pull Quotes** (e.g. *Austin Quote*, *Banded Quote*).
2. Format the quote in **14pt Italic**, set subtle top and bottom border rules, and center it between columns.
3. Configure Text Wrapping to **Square** or **Top and Bottom**.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is an oversized decorative capital letter at the beginning of a chapter called in typography?
A. Super Cap
B. Drop Cap
C. WordArt
D. Initial Cap
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Drop Cap (Dropped Capital) is an oversized initial letter spanning several lines of text.
---

### 2. Under which Ribbon tab and group is the "Drop Cap" command located?
A. Home > Font
B. Insert > Text group
C. Design > Themes
D. Layout > Page Setup
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Insert > Text group contains the Drop Cap, Text Box, and Quick Parts commands.
---

### 3. How many vertical lines of text does a standard Drop Cap span by default in Microsoft Word?
A. 1 line
B. 2 lines
C. 3 lines
D. 5 lines
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** By default, Word sets a Drop Cap to span 3 lines tall.
---

### 4. Which Drop Cap option pushes the oversized letter into the left margin outside the normal text boundary?
A. Dropped
B. In Margin
C. Outside Text
D. Floating Cap
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** "In Margin" positions the drop cap in the blank left margin buffer.
---

### 5. Can a Drop Cap letter have a completely different font family and color than the paragraph text that follows it?
A. No, it must share the paragraph font
B. Yes, font and color can be customized independently in Drop Cap Options and Font tools
C. Only in Photoshop
D. Only with VBA
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Drop Caps can be independently formatted with unique fonts, sizes, colors, and shadows.
