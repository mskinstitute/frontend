# Automation Techniques

## Content Automation: Quick Parts & AutoText

Typing the exact same multi-paragraph company disclaimer, executive bios, or complex mathematical formulas repeatedly wastes hours. Microsoft Word's **Quick Parts** and **Building Blocks** turn reusable content into instant 1-click snippets.

### 1. AutoText: Expanding Snippets via `F3`

AutoText stores formatted paragraphs, tables, or logos that expand from a short trigger code:
1. Select the content you frequently reuse (e.g. your complete 4-line email signature block with logo).
2. Go to **Insert** tab > **Text** group > click **Quick Parts > AutoText > Save Selection to AutoText Gallery...**
3. Name it with a unique short prefix (e.g. `mysig`).
4. Click **OK**.
5. *How to Use It*: Anywhere in any document, simply type `mysig` and press the **F3** function key! Word instantly swaps `mysig` with your full formatted signature and logo!

### 2. AutoCorrect: Replacing Abbreviations on Spacebar

For short text and mathematical symbols:
1. Go to **File > Options > Proofing > AutoCorrect Options...**
2. In the Replace box: Type `(c)` ➔ With box: `©` (built-in!).
3. Add your own:
   - Replace: `mski` ➔ With: `MSK Institute of Computer Technology`
   - Replace: `alpha` ➔ With: `α`
   - Replace: `!= ` ➔ With: `≠`
4. Click **Add**, then click **OK**.
5. Every time you type `mski` and hit the spacebar, Word expands it into the full institution title automatically!

### 3. Dynamic Document Property Fields

Keep document metadata synchronized without retyping:
1. Navigate to **Insert > Quick Parts > Document Property**:
   - Select: **Company**, **Author**, **Title**, or **Publish Date**.
2. Word inserts an interactive metadata field.
3. If you insert the `Title` property field on your Cover Page, in your Header, and in your Executive Summary, editing the title in **any one place** instantly updates all three locations automatically!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which function key expands an AutoText shortcut code (e.g. typing "mysig" and pressing this key) into full text?
A. F1
B. F3
C. F7
D. F12
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Pressing the F3 key expands AutoText and Building Block entries.
---

### 2. Under which Ribbon tab is the "Quick Parts" gallery located?
A. Home
B. Insert
C. References
D. Layout
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Insert > Text group houses the Quick Parts, AutoText, and Document Property tools.
---

### 3. What is the benefit of using "Document Property" fields (like Title or Author) from Quick Parts?
A. It locks the document with encryption
B. Updating the property in one place automatically updates all instances across the document
C. It translates text into Spanish
D. It turns text into a table
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Document Property fields sync globally, so updating the title on the cover updates headers everywhere.
---

### 4. Where in Word settings can you configure automated text replacement upon pressing the spacebar (e.g. mski -> MSK Institute)?
A. Review > Language
B. File > Options > Proofing > AutoCorrect Options
C. Design > Watermark
D. View > Split
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** AutoCorrect Options in Proofing settings handles automated keystroke replacements.
---

### 5. Where are custom Building Blocks and Quick Parts physically stored by default in Word?
A. In a plain text file on Desktop
B. Inside Built-In Building Blocks.dotx or Normal.dotm
C. In the Windows Registry
D. In temporary internet files
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Building Blocks are stored in template files like Building Blocks.dotx or Normal.dotm.
