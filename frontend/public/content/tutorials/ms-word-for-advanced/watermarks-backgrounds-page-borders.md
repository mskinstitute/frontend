# Watermarks, Backgrounds & Page Borders

## Brand Security & Accents: Watermarks & Backgrounds

Whether marking a legal contract as **CONFIDENTIAL**, a technical proposal as **DRAFT**, or adding an embossed corporate logo behind text, watermarks provide non-intrusive brand security.

### 1. Inserting Text Watermarks

1. Navigate to **Design** tab > **Page Background** group (far right) > click **Watermark**.
2. Select a quick preset:
   - *CONFIDENTIAL* (Diagonal or Horizontal)
   - *DO NOT COPY*
   - *DRAFT* / *URGENT*
3. The faint gray text appears diagonally across every page, sitting behind your body paragraphs and tables.

### 2. Customizing Watermarks (Logos & Custom Text)

Click **Design > Watermark > Custom Watermark...**:
- **Picture Watermark**:
  - Click **Select Picture...** and upload your company logo (PNG or SVG recommended).
  - **Scale**: Auto, 100%, 150%, or 200%.
  - **Washout Checkbox**:
    - Checked (Default): Lightens the logo into a subtle, low-opacity background watermark that does not interfere with reading.
    - Unchecked: Keeps the image's vibrant full-opacity colors.
- **Text Watermark**:
  - Customize text: e.g. `PROPERTY OF MSK INSTITUTE`.
  - Font, Size, Color (e.g. Light Red or Gray).
  - Layout: **Diagonal** vs. **Horizontal**.
  - **Semitransparent**: Keeps text faint and unobtrusive.

### 3. Removing Watermarks

- Go to **Design > Watermark > Remove Watermark**.
- *Under the Hood*: Watermarks in Word actually live inside the **Header** layer! If a watermark refuses to delete, double-click into the Header, click the watermark graphic/WordArt, and press `Delete`.

### 4. Page Background Color (Page Color)

- Go to **Design > Page Color**:
  - Choose a soft parchment tint, dark mode slate, or subtle gradient fill.
  - *Printing Note*: By default, Word does **not** print page background colors to save printer ink! To force Word to print backgrounds, go to **File > Options > Display > check "Print background colors and images"**.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Under which Ribbon tab is the "Watermark" command located in modern Microsoft Word?
A. Insert
B. Design
C. Layout
D. View
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Design tab > Page Background group contains Watermark, Page Color, and Page Borders.
---

### 2. In which underlying layer of a Word document do watermarks physically reside?
A. Footer layer only
B. The Header layer
C. The Footnotes layer
D. A hidden macro layer
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Watermarks are anchored inside the Header layer, allowing them to repeat across all pages.
---

### 3. What does the "Washout" checkbox do when creating a Picture Watermark in Word?
A. Blurs the photo completely
B. Fades the logo into a high-transparency subtle background so text remains legible
C. Inverts the colors
D. Converts it into text
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Washout reduces opacity and contrast so the background graphic does not obstruct body text.
---

### 4. Why does Word not print full-page background colors by default when sent to a physical printer?
A. It is a bug
B. To prevent accidental excessive ink and toner consumption
C. Printers only support white paper
D. Page colors are digital only
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Word disables background printing by default to conserve printer toner; it can be enabled in File > Options.
---

### 5. How can you manually delete a persistent watermark if the "Remove Watermark" button fails?
A. Clear formatting
B. Double-click into the Header, select the watermark object, and press Delete
C. Press Ctrl + Z
D. Save as PDF
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Double-clicking the Header exposes the watermark graphic directly, allowing manual deletion.
