# Presentation Design and Color Themes (प्रेजेंटेशन डिज़ाइन और थीम)

Designing an effective presentation is not just about adding text; it is an art of visual communication. A well-designed presentation enhances speaker credibility, conveys information clearly, and keeps the audience focused.

---

## 1. Contrast Rules for Legibility (कंट्रास्ट का नियम)

The golden rule of presentation typography is **High Contrast**:

```text
   [DARK ROOM / AUDITORIUM]                  [BRIGHT CLASSROOM / DAYLIGHT]
   Dark Background (Navy Blue/Black)         Light Background (White/Off-White)
              +                                          +
   Light Text (White/Bright Yellow)          Dark Text (Black/Dark Slate)
              =                                          =
      EXCELLENT CONTRAST                         EXCELLENT CONTRAST
```

> **The Red-Green Warning:** Never place red text over a green background (or vice versa)! Many individuals suffer from red-green color blindness, making such combinations completely unreadable.

---

## 2. Choosing Color Themes

A **Theme** is a pre-packaged harmonious palette containing:
- Complementary **Primary** and **Secondary** accent colors.
- Matched **Heading** and **Body text fonts**.
- Consistent slide background textures.

---

## 3. Best Practices for Professional Presentations

1. **The Rule of 3 Colors:** Limit your presentation to a maximum of three core colors (Background color, Primary text color, and one Accent highlight color for important keywords).
2. **San-Serif Fonts for Screens:** Use clean, unadorned **Sans-Serif fonts** (like Arial, Calibri, Trebuchet MS, or Liberation Sans) rather than curly serif fonts, as sans-serif letters are significantly easier to read on digital projectors from a distance.
3. **Avoid Clutter:** Never paste walls of tiny text. Replace dense paragraphs with visual diagrams, flowcharts, or bulleted key takeaways.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which font category is widely recommended for slide presentations due to its superior readability on digital projectors?
A. Decorative Cursive fonts
B. Sans-Serif fonts (e.g. Arial, Calibri)
C. Old English Gothic fonts
D. Monospaced code fonts
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Sans-serif typefaces (without decorative tail strokes) maintain sharp visual clarity on projector screens and digital monitors, making them ideal for presentations.

---

### 2. What color combination provides the highest visual contrast for reading in a brightly lit classroom?
A. Yellow text on white background
B. Dark black text on a clean white/light background
C. Red text on green background
D. Gray text on silver background
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** High contrast between dark text and a light background ensures maximum legibility under ambient daylight or bright classroom conditions.

---

### 3. What is a pre-coordinated set of background styles, colors, and font pairings called?
A. Theme
B. Macro
C. Formula
D. Bookmark
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A Theme is a curated collection of formatting elements including color palettes, font styles, and graphic backgrounds that establish unified visual aesthetics across a presentation.

---

### 4. Why should red text placed on a green background generally be avoided in presentations?
A. It causes computer crashes
B. It is difficult to read and causes problems for color-blind individuals
C. It consumes double printer ink
D. It is illegal in government offices
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Red-green color blindness is the most common visual deficiency; placing red on green creates severe visual vibration and illegibility for many viewers.

---

### 5. According to professional presentation design standards, what is the maximum number of bullet points recommended per slide?
A. 20 to 25
B. 5 to 6
C. Exactly 1
D. Unlimited
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The "6x6 Rule" advises limiting slides to approximately 5 to 6 bullet points, keeping slides clean, digestible, and focused.
