# UPI, AEPS, and eWallet Concepts (डिजिटल भुगतान प्रणाली)

India leads the world in real-time digital payment volume, accounting for nearly 46% of all global digital transactions! 

This revolution is powered by indigenous platforms developed by the **National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)**: **UPI** (Unified Payments Interface) and **AEPS** (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System).

---

## 1. What is UPI? (Unified Payments Interface - यूपीआई)

**UPI** is an advanced, real-time payment system developed by **NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India)** and regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). It was officially launched in **April 2016** by Dr. Raghuram Rajan.

### Core Features of UPI:
1. **Instant 24x7 Interbank Transfer:** Transfer money from your bank to any other bank account instantly (365 days a year, including bank holidays and Sundays!).
2. **Virtual Payment Address (VPA / UPI ID):**
   - You don't need to share your sensitive bank account number or branch IFSC code!
   - You only share your **UPI ID (VPA)**, which looks like an email address:
     - *Example:* `sumit@sbi`, `mobileNumber@paytm`, `user@okhdfcbank`.
3. **Authentication via UPI PIN:**
   - Transactions are secured with a private **4-digit or 6-digit numeric UPI PIN** that you set during registration.
   - **Crucial Rule:** You enter your UPI PIN **ONLY when SENDING / PAYING money**. **You NEVER enter a UPI PIN to RECEIVE money!**
4. **Transaction Limits:** Standard UPI transfer limit is typically **₹1,00,000 per day** (higher for tax payments, hospitals, and educational institutions).

---

## 2. What is AEPS? (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System)

**AEPS** is a bank-led model developed by NPCI that allows interoperable financial transactions at **Micro-ATMs (PoS terminals)** using a citizen's **Aadhaar authentication**.

### Why AEPS is Revolutionary for Rural India:
- In remote villages of Uttar Pradesh, citizens don't need to walk 10 kilometers to reach a physical bank branch or ATM.
- A local **Banking Correspondent (Bank Mitra / Grahak Seva Kendra)** carries a portable smartphone and biometric fingerprint scanner.
- **Requirements for AEPS:**
  1. The citizen's bank account must be **linked with their Aadhaar Number**.
  2. The citizen's Bank Name.
  3. **Biometric Fingerprint or Iris scan** (No ATM card, debit card, or phone number required!).
- **Services on AEPS:**
  - Cash Withdrawal (नकद निकासी)
  - Cash Deposit (नकद जमा)
  - Balance Enquiry (खाता शेष जांच)
  - Mini-Statement (अंतिम 10 लेनदेन)
  - Aadhaar to Aadhaar Fund Transfer

---

## 3. What is an e-Wallet (Digital Wallet)?

An **e-Wallet** (Electronic Wallet) is a digital prepaid storage container on your smartphone where you can load money from your bank account or debit card in advance to make fast purchases (e.g. Paytm Wallet, Mobikwik, Amazon Pay Balance).
- **Difference from UPI:** In UPI, money moves **directly from Bank Account to Bank Account**. In an e-Wallet, money is stored in an intermediate virtual wallet balance.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which organization developed the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) and AEPS in India?
A. NITI Aayog
B. NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India)
C. SEBI
D. Ministry of Finance
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India), an initiative of RBI and IBA, architected and operates UPI, AEPS, RuPay, IMPS, and FASTag.

---

### 2. Do you need to enter your UPI PIN to RECEIVE money into your bank account?
A. Yes, always enter UPI PIN to receive money
B. No, UPI PIN is entered ONLY when sending/debiting money from your account
C. Only for amounts above ₹5,000
D. Only on Sundays
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** UPI PIN is strictly an authorization code required to debit/send funds from your account. Receiving funds requires no PIN; scammers often trick victims into entering their PIN to "receive" money, which actually debits their account!

---

### 3. Which three credentials are required to withdraw cash at a rural Micro-ATM using AEPS?
A. Credit card, CVV, and OTP
B. Bank Name, Aadhaar Number, and Biometric Fingerprint
C. Pan Card, Driving License, and Signature
D. Cheque book, Passbook, and Photo
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** AEPS requires only the customer's Bank Name, 12-digit Aadhaar Number, and biometric fingerprint/iris authentication verified against the UIDAI database.

---

### 4. What is a Virtual Payment Address (VPA) in UPI?
A. A physical bank branch location
B. A unique identifier that acts as a financial address (e.g. `name@bank`) without revealing bank account numbers
C. A 16-digit debit card number
D. The bank manager's email address
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A VPA (UPI ID) is a unique virtual financial address that tracks payments directly to your linked bank account without disclosing sensitive account or branch IFSC details.

---

### 5. In which year was the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) officially launched in India?
A. 2010
B. 2014
C. 2016
D. 2020
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** UPI was publicly dedicated to the nation on 11 April 2016 by RBI Governor Dr. Raghuram Rajan.
