# e-Governance Applications: Passport, Railway, eHospital (ई-गवर्नेंस सेवाएं)

**e-Governance (Electronic Governance)** is the application of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) by the government to deliver public services, exchange information, and conduct transactions with citizens in a transparent, speedy, and efficient manner.

Under the flagship **Digital India** initiative, citizens can access vital government services 24x7 from their mobile phones or local **CSC (Common Service Centres - जन सेवा केंद्र)** without visiting government offices.

---

## 1. The 4 Models of e-Governance

The interactions in e-Governance are classified into four sectors:
1. **G2C (Government to Citizen):** Services provided directly to the public (e.g. applying for a Passport, UP Bhulekh land records, voter ID cards).
2. **G2B (Government to Business):** Services between government and commercial enterprises (e.g. GST filing, corporate taxes, e-Tendering).
3. **G2G (Government to Government):** Digital communication between different government departments and ministries.
4. **G2E (Government to Employee):** Services provided to government servants (e.g. employee provident fund passbook, digital salary slips).

---

## 2. Major Indian Citizen Portals (CCC Super Important)

### A. IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation)
- Official web portal: **`irctc.co.in`**
- Book reserved train tickets, check **PNR Status** (Passenger Name Record - 10-digit number), inspect train schedules, and book Retiring Rooms.
- Features **Tatkal** ticket booking for urgent travel.

### B. Passport Seva Portal (पासपोर्ट सेवा)
- Official web portal: **`passportindia.gov.in`**
- Operated by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA).
- Apply online for fresh passports, submit fees, and schedule biometric appointments at your local **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** or Post Office PSK (POPSK).

### C. e-Hospital & eSanjeevani (स्वास्थ्य सेवाएं)
- **e-Hospital:** An online portal by NIC connecting government hospitals across India. Citizens can register online, obtain OPD appointment slips, and view diagnostic blood test reports digitally.
- **eSanjeevani:** Free telemedicine consultation service by the Ministry of Health allowing rural citizens to consult specialized doctors via video call.

### D. National Scholarship Portal (NSP - `scholarships.gov.in`)
- Single unified national portal where pre-matric and post-matric students can apply for Central and State government educational scholarships with direct bank transfer (**DBT**).

### E. e-District Uttar Pradesh (`edistrict.up.gov.in`)
- Dedicated portal for UP citizens to obtain verified digital certificates:
  - **Income Certificate (आय प्रमाण पत्र)**
  - **Caste Certificate (जाति प्रमाण पत्र)**
  - **Domicile Certificate (निवास प्रमाण पत्र)**
  - **Birth & Death Certificates (जन्म और मृत्यु प्रमाण पत्र)**

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What does the acronym "PNR" on an Indian Railway train ticket stand for?
A. Passenger Name Record
B. Public Network Route
C. Permanent Number Reservation
D. Passenger Number Receipt
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** PNR stands for Passenger Name Record. It is a unique 10-digit database identifier that stores a passenger's itinerary, coach, and seat status on Indian Railways.

---

### 2. Under which e-Governance category does a citizen applying for an Income Certificate online fall?
A. G2G (Government to Government)
B. G2C (Government to Citizen)
C. G2B (Government to Business)
D. B2B (Business to Business)
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** G2C (Government to Citizen) encompasses all public government services, applications, and documents provided directly to individual citizens.

---

### 3. Which official government portal handles online Indian passport applications and appointment booking?
A. `passportindia.gov.in`
B. `irctc.co.in`
C. `uidai.gov.in`
D. `upsssc.gov.in`
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** `passportindia.gov.in` (Passport Seva) is the official portal of the Ministry of External Affairs for passport applications and verification.

---

### 4. What is the National Telemedicine service launched by the Government of India for online doctor consultations called?
A. Aarogya Setu
B. eSanjeevani
C. Swachh Bharat
D. CoWIN
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** eSanjeevani is the national doctor-to-patient telemedicine platform enabling free virtual medical consultations from home.

---

### 5. How many digits are present in an Indian Railways PNR number?
A. 8 Digits
B. 10 Digits
C. 12 Digits
D. 16 Digits
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Every Indian Railways reservation ticket features a unique 10-digit PNR (Passenger Name Record) printed in the top-left corner.
