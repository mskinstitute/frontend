# Creating and Managing an Email Account (ईमेल खाता बनाना और प्रबंधन)

**E-mail (Electronic Mail)** is the digital method of exchanging messages, letters, and multimedia files between people across the internet. 

Invented in 1971 by **Ray Tomlinson** (who also introduced the **`@`** symbol for email addresses), email has replaced traditional postal letters for all formal communication, job applications, and official government correspondence.

---

## 1. Anatomy of an Email Address (ईमेल पता संरचना)

An email address consists of two primary components separated by the **`@` (at the rate)** symbol:

```text
                 sumit.sharma @ gmail.com
                 ─────┬──────   ────┬────
                      │             │
                1. Username     2. Domain Name
```

1. **Username (यूज़रनेम):** The unique handle chosen by the account owner (e.g. `sumit.sharma` or `mskinstitute`).
2. **Domain Name (डोमेन नाम):** The email service provider hosting the mailbox (e.g. `gmail.com`, `yahoo.com`, `outlook.com`).
- **Important Rules:** Email addresses are **NOT case-sensitive** (`SUMIT@GMAIL.COM` is identical to `sumit@gmail.com`) and **CANNOT contain spaces**!

---

## 2. Main Folders in an Email Mailbox

When you log into your email account, your dashboard is organized into standard folders:

- **Inbox (इनबॉक्स):** The primary folder where all incoming received emails arrive. Unread emails appear in **Bold font**.
- **Sent (सेंट):** Stores copies of all messages that you have successfully dispatched to others.
- **Drafts (ड्राफ्ट्स):** Stores incomplete messages that you started composing but have not yet sent. Saved automatically so you don't lose your work!
- **Spam / Junk (स्पैम):** Automatic quarantine folder holding unsolicited commercial advertisements, phishing scams, and bulk junk emails. Spam is automatically deleted after 30 days.
- **Trash / Bin (ट्रैश / कचरा):** Stores deleted emails temporarily. If you accidentally delete an important email, you can restore it from Trash within 30 days before it is permanently erased!
- **Starred / Important:** Allows you to mark critical messages with a yellow star for instant retrieval.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Who is credited with inventing electronic mail (email) and introducing the `@` symbol in 1971?
A. Tim Berners-Lee
B. Ray Tomlinson
C. Charles Babbage
D. Steve Jobs
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Ray Tomlinson implemented the first ARPANET networked email system in 1971 and selected the `@` symbol to separate the user from their host machine.

---

### 2. Can an email address contain space characters (e.g. `sumit sharma@gmail.com`)?
A. Yes, spaces are allowed anywhere
B. No, email addresses strictly prohibit spaces
C. Only in the domain name
D. Only in Yahoo mail
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Standard Internet email protocols (RFC 5322) strictly prohibit whitespace characters within an email address identifier.

---

### 3. In which folder are incomplete or unsent email drafts automatically preserved?
A. Outbox
B. Drafts
C. Sent
D. Spam
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The "Drafts" folder automatically retains composed messages that have not yet been transmitted, enabling users to resume editing later.

---

### 4. What happens to messages moved to the "Trash / Bin" folder in Gmail?
A. They are sent back to the sender
B. They are automatically and permanently deleted after 30 days
C. They remain forever until hard drive fills up
D. They are printed automatically
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Modern email providers automatically and permanently purge deleted items residing in the Trash folder after a 30-day retention window.

---

### 5. What type of messages are automatically filtered into the "Spam / Junk" folder?
A. Official government notices
B. Unsolicited commercial advertising, suspicious phishing links, and bulk scam emails
C. Password reset codes
D. Draft letters
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Intelligent mail server filters redirect unsolicited promotional broadcasts, malware distribution attempts, and suspicious phishing messages into the Spam folder.
