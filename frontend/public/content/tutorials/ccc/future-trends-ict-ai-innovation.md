### 1. High-Speed Telecommunications (5G and the Road to 6G)

The transition from early 2G voice networks to 5G has enabled real-time global connectivity:

| Cellular Generation | Era & Speed | Primary Capability & Landmark Innovation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1G** (1980s) | ~2.4 Kbps | Analog voice calls only; large mobile handsets. |
| **2G** (1990s) | ~64 Kbps | Digital voice calls, SMS text messages, and basic GPRS. |
| **3G** (2000s) | ~2 Mbps | Mobile internet browsing, MMS, and video calling on basic smartphones. |
| **4G LTE** (2010s) | ~100 Mbps | High-definition (HD) video streaming, UPI payments, and the app economy. |
| **5G** (2020s) | **1 Gbps to 10 Gbps** | Ultra-Low Latency (<1 ms), massive machine IoT communication, robotic surgeries. |
| **6G** (Expected 2030) | **Up to 1 Tbps** | Terahertz spectrum, holographic communication, AI-native network control. |

### 2. Quantum Computing: Beyond Binary Bits

Traditional computers (from smartphones to supercomputers) process data using classical **Bits**, which can exist in only one of two states at any given moment: **0 or 1**.

**Quantum Computers** harness the bizarre principles of quantum physics:
- **Qubits (Quantum Bits):** Unlike classical bits, qubits can exist in a state of **Superposition**—meaning they can represent **both 0 and 1 simultaneously**.
- **Entanglement:** Qubits can be interconnected in ways that allow calculating exponentially vast numbers of possibilities in parallel.
- **Real-World Impact:** Simulating new pharmaceutical molecular drugs in days rather than decades, breaking current encryption standards, and optimizing complex global supply chains.

### 3. Blockchain Technology & Digital Trust

Originally invented as the underlying ledger for Bitcoin, **Blockchain** is a distributed, decentralized, and immutable digital ledger:

- **Decentralized (Vikendrikrit):** No single bank, government, or corporation owns the central ledger; data is replicated across thousands of independent nodes across the world.
- **Immutable (Aparivartaniya):** Once a transaction block is verified and linked using cryptographic hashes, it can never be altered or deleted.
- **Applications:** Land property registry (Bhulekh) with tamper-proof records, digital degree certificates that cannot be forged, and transparent voting systems.

### 4. India's Digital Leadership: The India Stack

India has emerged as an undisputed global leader in public digital infrastructure through the **India Stack**:

1. **Identity Layer (Aadhaar):** Over 135+ crore biometric digital identities providing instant e-KYC.
2. **Payments Layer (UPI):** Processing over 10-15 billion real-time financial transactions each month with zero merchant discount rates on basic transfers.
3. **Data Governance (DigiLocker & Account Aggregator):** Paperless verification of marks sheets, vehicle registration, and PAN cards directly on smartphones.
4. **ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce):** Democratizing e-commerce so small neighborhood kirana shops can compete with multinational retail giants.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is the fundamental unit of information in a Quantum Computer that can represent 0 and 1 simultaneously?
A. Byte
B. Qubit (Quantum Bit)
C. Nibble
D. Pixel
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Qubit (quantum bit) is the basic unit of quantum information, capable of existing in multiple states simultaneously due to quantum superposition.
---

### 2. Which feature of Blockchain technology ensures that records once written can never be altered or deleted?
A. Volatility
B. Immutability
C. Slicing
D. Defragmentation
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Immutability means that once transaction data is cryptographically confirmed and added to a blockchain, it cannot be modified or erased.
---

### 3. What key advantage does 5G cellular technology provide over 4G networks besides higher bandwidth?
A. Requires bigger SIM cards
B. Ultra-low latency (response time under 1 millisecond)
C. Completely free international calls
D. Eliminates the need for mobile towers
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** 5G provides ultra-low latency (<1ms delay), which is crucial for autonomous self-driving vehicles, remote surgeries, and high-speed industrial robotics.
---

### 4. What is 'ONDC' launched by the Government of India to democratize e-commerce?
A. Online National Degree Certificate
B. Open Network for Digital Commerce
C. Optical Network for Direct Communication
D. Overseas Non-resident Debit Card
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce) is an initiative by the Government of India to create an open, interoperable network for digital buying and selling.

### 5. Which of the following represents the foundational digital identity layer in India's public digital infrastructure (India Stack)?
A. Voter ID
B. Aadhaar (UIDAI)
C. Ration Card
D. Driving License
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Aadhaar provides the 12-digit biometric digital identity verification layer that anchors the entire India Stack ecosystem.
