# Social Media Basics: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (सोशल मीडिया परिचय)

**Social Media** refers to internet-based interactive digital platforms that enable individuals, businesses, and government departments to create, share, and exchange information, photos, videos, and career resumes across global online communities.

---

## 1. Major Social Networking Platforms

### A. Facebook (Meta)
- Founded in **2004** by **Mark Zuckerberg** at Harvard University.
- The largest social network on earth with over 3 billion active users.
- Connects friends, family, alumni groups, and local business pages.
- Features: Timeline, News Feed, Groups, Marketplace, Stories.

### B. Instagram (Meta)
- Acquired by Facebook in 2012.
- A visual-first mobile platform focused predominantly on **Photos, Short-form Videos (Reels)**, and ephemeral 24-hour **Stories**.
- Popular among youth and businesses for visual brand storytelling.

### C. LinkedIn (Microsoft)
- The world's premier **Professional Career & Job Networking Platform** (acquired by Microsoft in 2016).
- Used by professionals, students, recruiters, and companies:
  - Create a digital professional resume (Education, Certifications like CCC, Skills).
  - Apply for corporate and government contractor jobs.
  - Connect with industry mentors and HR managers.

### D. X (Formerly Twitter)
- Microblogging platform founded in 2006 by Jack Dorsey; acquired by Elon Musk in 2022.
- Characterized by short, fast-paced updates called **Posts (Tweets)**:
  - Standard account character limit: **280 characters**.
  - Uses the hashtag symbol (**`#`**) to aggregate discussions around trending news topics (e.g. `#DigitalIndia`, `#CCCExam`).
  - Used by Indian Government ministries (PMO, Railways, UP Police) for official press releases and citizen grievance redressal.

---

## 2. Social Media Safety Rules (सुरक्षा नियम)

Social media is a public forum. Careless sharing can compromise your privacy and safety:
1. **Never Share Sensitive Personal Data:** Never post your Aadhaar number, PAN card photo, home address, or debit card on social media!
2. **Lock Your Profile:** Enable privacy settings so only trusted friends can view your personal photos.
3. **Beware of Fake Profiles (Catfishing):** Do not accept friend requests from unknown strangers pretending to offer instant jobs or money.
4. **Think Before You Post:** Digital footprints are permanent; offensive posts can lead to legal penalties under the **IT Act, 2000**.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which social networking platform is designed specifically for professional employment, career resumes, and job recruiting?
A. Instagram
B. LinkedIn
C. Snapchat
D. Pinterest
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** LinkedIn is the world's leading business and employment-oriented platform used by professionals for career networking, sharing resumes, and recruiting.

---

### 2. What is the standard character limit for a standard post on X (formerly Twitter)?
A. 140 characters
B. 280 characters
C. 500 characters
D. Unlimited
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** While Twitter originally launched with a 140-character limit, it expanded the standard post limit to 280 characters in 2017.

---

### 3. Which symbol is universally used on social media platforms to tag trending topics and keywords?
A. `@` (At the rate)
B. `#` (Hashtag)
C. `$` (Dollar)
D. `&` (Ampersand)
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The hashtag symbol (`#`) is used across social media platforms to categorize posts, aggregate conversations, and track trending topics.

---

### 4. Who founded Facebook in 2004?
A. Bill Gates
B. Mark Zuckerberg
C. Jeff Bezos
D. Larry Page
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Mark Zuckerberg co-founded Facebook in February 2004 from his Harvard University dorm room.

---

### 5. Which company currently owns and operates both Instagram and WhatsApp?
A. Microsoft
B. Google
C. Meta (formerly Facebook Inc.)
D. Apple
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Meta Platforms Inc. (formerly Facebook Inc.) is the parent corporate entity that owns Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger.
