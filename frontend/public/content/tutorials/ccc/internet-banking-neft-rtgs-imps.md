# Internet Banking: NEFT, RTGS, IMPS (इंटरनेट बैंकिंग और फंड ट्रांसफर)

**Internet Banking** (also known as Online Banking or Net Banking) is an electronic payment system that enables customers of a bank to conduct financial and non-financial transactions through the bank's secure website.

When transferring money electronically between different banks in India, the RBI offers three standard electronic transfer channels: **NEFT**, **RTGS**, and **IMPS**.

---

## 1. Comparing NEFT vs RTGS vs IMPS (CCC Exam Core Question)

Memorize this comparison table thoroughly—these exact specifications appear in every NIELIT examination:

| Parameter | NEFT | RTGS | IMPS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Full Form** | **National Electronic Funds Transfer** | **Real Time Gross Settlement** | **Immediate Payment Service** |
| **Operating Organization** | **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** | **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** | **NPCI** |
| **Settlement Nature** | **Deferred Net Settlement in Half-Hourly Batches** | **Gross Settlement (Real-Time instantaneous)** | **Instant Real-Time (Immediate)** |
| **Minimum Transfer Limit** | **₹1 (No Minimum Limit)** | **₹2,00,000 (Two Lakhs Minimum!)** | **₹1 (No Minimum Limit)** |
| **Maximum Transfer Limit** | No upper ceiling | No upper ceiling | Usually ₹5,00,000 per day |
| **Operational Timing** | **24x7x365 Days** | **24x7x365 Days** | **24x7x365 Days** |
| **Primary Usage** | General utility and personal transfers | High-value commercial business payments | Instant mobile phone/account transfers |

## 2. What is an IFSC Code? (आईएफएससी कोड)

To route money to the correct bank and branch across India, every electronic transfer requires an **IFSC (Indian Financial System Code)**:
- An **11-character alphanumeric code** assigned by the RBI to every bank branch.
- **Anatomy of an IFSC Code:**
  ```text
  S B I N 0 0 0 1 2 3 4
  ──┬──── ┬ ─────┬─────
    │     │      │
  1.Bank  2.Zero 3.Branch Code
  ```
  1. **First 4 Characters (Alphabetic):** Represents the Bank Name (e.g. `SBIN` = State Bank of India, `PUNB` = Punjab National Bank).
  2. **Fifth Character:** **ALWAYS THE NUMBER ZERO (`0`)** reserved for future use!
  3. **Last 6 Characters (Alphanumeric):** Identifies the specific bank branch office.

---

## 3. What is an MMID? (Mobile Money Identifier)
- A **7-digit numeric code** issued by a bank to customers using mobile banking.
- Combined with a mobile phone number, it allows receiving IMPS fund transfers without sharing an account number.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is the minimum transaction amount required to transfer funds through RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)?
A. ₹1
B. ₹10,000
C. ₹1,00,000
D. ₹2,00,000
**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** RTGS is engineered specifically for large-value transfers; the RBI has established a strict minimum threshold of ₹2,00,000 (Two Lakhs) per transaction.

---

### 2. How many total characters are present in an Indian Financial System Code (IFSC)?
A. 9 Characters
B. 10 Characters
C. 11 Characters
D. 16 Characters
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** An IFSC code consists of exactly 11 alphanumeric characters (4 bank letters, a control '0', and 6 branch alphanumeric characters).

---

### 3. What is the 5th character of every valid IFSC code in India?
A. Any letter from A-Z
B. Always the number zero (`0`)
C. The number nine (`9`)
D. A hyphen (`-`)
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** By RBI design standard, the 5th character of every 11-digit IFSC code is permanently fixed as the number zero (`0`).

---

### 4. How are fund transfers settled under NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)?
A. Once every 24 hours at midnight
B. In half-hourly batches 24 hours a day, 365 days a year
C. Only on Monday mornings
D. Only during physical bank open hours
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** NEFT processes electronic funds transfers in half-hourly batches round-the-clock (24x7x365) throughout the year.

---

### 5. How many digits are contained in a Mobile Money Identifier (MMID) used for IMPS mobile banking?
A. 4 Digits
B. 6 Digits
C. 7 Digits
D. 10 Digits
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** An MMID (Mobile Money Identifier) is a 7-digit unique numeric code generated by banks for customers enrolled in mobile banking.
