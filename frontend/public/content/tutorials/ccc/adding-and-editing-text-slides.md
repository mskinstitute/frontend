# Adding and Editing Text on Slides (स्लाइड्स पर टेक्स्ट जोड़ना)

Unlike a blank page in a word processor where you can click and start typing anywhere, you **cannot type directly on the background canvas of a slide**. 

In presentation software, all text must be typed inside specialized containers called **Placeholders** or **Text Boxes** (टेक्स्ट बॉक्स).

---

## 1. What are Placeholders? (प्लेसहोल्डर क्या हैं?)

**Placeholders** are dotted-line bounding boxes on a slide layout that designate where titles, body bullet points, tables, or pictures should reside.

Standard placeholders include:
- **Title Placeholder (शीर्षक):** Contains large, prominent font designed for the main slide title (e.g. *"Types of Computer Networks"*).
- **Subtitle / Content Placeholder:** Contains body text formatted automatically as bullet points.

To add text, simply click inside the placeholder box where it says *"Click to add text"* and begin typing!

---

## 2. Inserting Custom Text Boxes

If you need to add an extra caption, callout, or label outside the pre-set placeholders:
- In **LibreOffice Impress**: Press the **`F2`** function key!
  - Clicking **`F2`** activates the Text Box tool immediately. Click and drag anywhere on the slide to draw a text container.
- In **MS PowerPoint**: Click **Insert Tab $\implies$ Text Box**.

---

## 3. Formatting Text on Slides

- **Bullet Points & Numbered Lists:**
  - Bulleted lists make complex concepts easy to scan for audiences.
  - To demote a bullet point to a secondary sub-bullet: Press **`Tab`**.
  - To promote a sub-bullet back to a primary bullet: Press **`Shift + Tab`**.
- **Font Size Guidelines for Presentations (The 6x6 Rule):**
  - Slide text must be readable from the back row of an auditorium.
  - Main Slide Titles: **`36 pt to 44 pt`**.
  - Body Bullet Points: **`24 pt to 32 pt`**. Never use tiny fonts below 18 pt!
  - *The 6x6 Rule:* Aim for maximum **6 bullet points per slide**, and maximum **6 words per bullet point** to keep slides uncluttered!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which function key activates the Text Box tool directly in LibreOffice Impress?
A. `F1`
B. `F2`
C. `F5`
D. `F7`
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** In LibreOffice Impress, pressing `F2` is the direct keyboard shortcut to activate the Text tool to create a new text box on the slide.

---

### 2. Can text be typed directly onto the blank background of a presentation slide without a container?
A. Yes, anywhere
B. No, text must always reside inside a placeholder or text box
C. Only in black-and-white mode
D. Only on the first slide
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** In presentation applications like Impress and PowerPoint, text cannot be placed directly onto the bare canvas; it must reside within placeholders, shapes, or text boxes.

---

### 3. What keyboard key demotes a bullet point to an indented sub-bullet level?
A. `Enter`
B. `Tab`
C. `Shift + Tab`
D. `Spacebar`
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Pressing the `Tab` key at the start of a bullet line indents it to a lower hierarchical sub-bullet level. `Shift + Tab` outdents it back.

---

### 4. What is the recommended minimum font size for body text on presentation slides to maintain readability?
A. 8 pt
B. 12 pt
C. 24 pt
D. 72 pt
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Body text on presentation slides should generally be at least 24 to 28 points so that audiences sitting at a distance can comfortably read the content.

---

### 5. What are the dotted-line containers on a slide layout designed to hold titles and text called?
A. Placeholders
B. Margins
C. Binders
D. Layers
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Placeholders are pre-formatted bounding boxes on slide layouts that hold text, titles, lists, charts, and media items.
