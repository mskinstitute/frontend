# Page Setup, Header, Footer, and Page Numbering (पेज सेटअप और हेडर/फुटर)

Before printing official government forms or college projects, you must configure page geometry: margins, paper size, page orientation, and automatic header/footer page numbers.

---

## 1. Page Orientation (पेज ओरिएंटेशन)

**Page Orientation** refers to the visual direction in which a document is displayed and printed:

1. **Portrait (लंबवत - डिफ़ॉल्ट):**
   - The page is taller than it is wide (Height > Width).
   - Standard for letters, novels, applications, and official government memos.
2. **Landscape (क्षैतिज / आड़ा):**
   - The page is wider than it is tall (Width > Height).
   - Standard for wide financial spreadsheets, large accounting tables, certificates, and presentation slides.

---

## 2. Standard Paper Sizes

Printers support various paper trays:
- **A4 Size (सबसे लोकप्रिय):** The international standard sheet for office printing ($210 \text{ mm} \times 297 \text{ mm}$ or $8.27" \times 11.69"$).
- **Letter Size:** Standard in the USA ($8.5" \times 11"$).
- **Legal Size:** Taller sheet used in Indian courts and legal stamp papers ($8.5" \times 14"$).

---

## 3. Page Margins (पेज मार्जिन)

A **Margin** is the blank white border area between the edge of the paper and the actual typed text.
- Standard margins are: **Top**, **Bottom**, **Left**, and **Right** (typically set to 0.75" or 1.0").
- **Gutter Margin (गटर मार्जिन):** An additional extra margin space added to the **Left side** (or Top) of pages specifically reserved for book binding, stapling, or hole punching, ensuring no printed text gets concealed inside the bound fold.

---

## 4. Header, Footer, and Automated Page Numbering

- **Header (हेडर - सबसे ऊपर):**
  - Content that appears automatically in the top margin of **every single page** throughout the document.
  - Typical contents: Book Title, Chapter Name, Institute Logo.
- **Footer (फुटर - सबसे नीचे):**
  - Content that appears automatically in the bottom margin of every page.
  - Typical contents: Author Name, Confidentiality Disclaimer, Date.
- **Automated Page Numbering (पृष्ठ संख्या):**
  - Never type page numbers manually! If you insert or delete paragraphs, manual numbers will become wrong.
  - In LibreOffice Writer: Click inside Header/Footer $\implies$ Click **Insert $\implies$ Field $\implies$ Page Number** (or Page Count). The software automatically increments numbers sequentially ($1, 2, 3...$).

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What are the two types of Page Orientation available in word processors?
A. Front and Back
B. Portrait and Landscape
C. Vertical and Diagonal
D. Top and Bottom
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The two standard page orientations are Portrait (vertical tall layout, which is the default) and Landscape (horizontal wide layout).

---

### 2. What is the standard physical dimension of an A4 size paper sheet?
A. $210 	imes 297 	ext{ mm}$
B. $150 	imes 200 	ext{ mm}$
C. $8.5 	imes 14 	ext{ inches}$
D. $300 	imes 400 	ext{ mm}$
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** International standard A4 paper measures exactly $210 	imes 297 	ext{ millimeters}$ ($8.27 	imes 11.69 	ext{ inches}$).

---

### 3. What is the extra margin added to the binding side of a document called?
A. Boundary Margin
B. Gutter Margin
C. Header Margin
D. Safe Zone
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Gutter Margin adds extra blank space to the inside/binding edge of pages so that printed text is not obscured when the pages are bound or stapled together into a book.

---

### 4. In which margin does the "Header" appear by default?
A. Bottom margin of odd pages only
B. Top margin of every page
C. Left margin
D. Center of the page
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The Header is printed in the top margin area of every page in the document or section.

---

### 5. How should page numbers be inserted in LibreOffice Writer so they update dynamically?
A. By manually typing "1", "2", "3" at the bottom of each page
B. By using Insert > Field > Page Number
C. By drawing a text box on every page
D. Page numbers cannot be added in Writer
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Inserting a dynamic Page Number field via "Insert > Field > Page Number" ensures that the word processor calculates and updates the page numbering automatically as text expands or shrinks.
