# Rehearse Timings, Speaker Coach & Recording Slide Shows

Polishing your vocal delivery, staying strictly within allocated time limits, and producing self-running narrated video presentations are capabilities that set elite presenters apart. PowerPoint includes **Rehearse Timings**, the AI-powered **Speaker Coach**, and an executive-grade **Recording Studio**.

---

## Rehearse Timings: Mastering Your Pace

Whether delivering a 5-minute lightning talk or a 20-minute venture pitch, running out of time is catastrophic:
1. Go to the **Slide Show** tab (or **Record** tab).
2. Click **Rehearse Timings**.
3. The presentation launches into full screen with a floating **Recording toolbar** in the top-left corner:
   - **Slide Timer**: Counts seconds spent on the current active slide.
   - **Total Presentation Timer**: Accumulates cumulative talk time.
   - **Pause / Repeat Button**: Lets you pause the timer or reset the current slide's timer if you stumble over your words.
4. Speak your presentation aloud and press the spacebar to advance naturally.
5. At the conclusion, PowerPoint displays a summary dialog: *"The total time for your slide show was 14:32. Do you want to save the new slide timings?"*.
6. Click **Yes**. PowerPoint saves the exact speaking durations as automatic slide advance timings in Slide Sorter View!

---

## PowerPoint Speaker Coach (AI Presentation Mentor)

Available in modern Microsoft 365 and PowerPoint for the Web:
1. Go to **Slide Show > Rehearse with Coach**.
2. Click **Start Rehearsing** and enable your computer microphone.
3. As you speak, PowerPoint's on-device AI listens in real time, delivering gentle pop-up coaching on your screen:
   - **Pacing**: Alerts you if you are speaking too fast (>160 words/min) or too slowly (<100 words/min).
   - **Filler Words**: Counts instances of "um", "ah", "like", "you know", "basically".
   - **Pitch & Monotone**: Evaluates vocal modulation to prevent monotone delivery.
   - **Originality (Slide Reading)**: Alerts you if you are simply reading slide bullet points verbatim instead of speaking organically.
   - **Sensitive Phrases**: Flags culturally insensitive or non-inclusive terminology.
4. When finished, Speaker Coach generates a comprehensive **Rehearsal Report dashboard** summarizing your performance metrics.

## The Recording Studio (Narrated Decks & Video)

On the **Record** tab, click **Record > From Beginning**:
- **Full Studio Interface**: Displays teleprompter notes, audio waveform meters, and webcam overlay controls.
- **Webcam & Mic Configuration**: Choose your external USB microphone and camera. You can position your live circular or rectangular talking-head camera preview in any corner of the slide!
- **Teleprompter View**: Displays your speaker notes in large scrolling text directly below your webcam lens, ensuring you maintain eye contact with the camera.
- **Blur Background**: Automatically blurs your real-world room background behind your camera cutout.
- **Exporting**: When recording concludes, click **Export > Export to Video** to render a 1080p or 4K `.mp4` video complete with your synchronized voiceover and animations!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What does PowerPoint's "Rehearse Timings" feature save when you click "Yes" at the conclusion of your practice session?
A. A video recording of your screen
B. The exact time spent speaking on each individual slide as automated slide advance timings
C. A transcript of your speech in Word
D. A bill for cloud computing
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Rehearse Timings records the duration spent on each slide and saves those durations into the presentation's automatic advance properties.

---

### 2. Which AI-powered feature in Microsoft 365 analyzes your vocal cadence, flags filler words ("um", "like"), and warns you if you are reading slides verbatim?
A. Rehearse with Coach (Speaker Coach)
B. Design Ideas
C. Smart Lookup
D. Morph Inspector
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Speaker Coach (Rehearse with Coach) uses speech recognition AI to deliver real-time feedback on pacing, pitch, filler words, and slide reading.

---

### 3. Where does the Teleprompter view in the PowerPoint Recording Studio display your speaker notes to ensure optimal eye contact?
A. At the bottom of your feet
B. Directly across the top of your screen near your laptop webcam lens
C. On the audience's projection screen
D. In a separate printed binder
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The Teleprompter view renders notes near the top edge of the monitor just below the webcam, allowing presenters to read notes while looking straight into the lens.

---

### 4. What output file format is produced when you export a recorded slide show from the Record tab?
A. .html
B. .mp4
C. .zip
D. .exe
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Recorded presentations with voice narration, timings, and video feeds export as standard high-definition MP4 video files.

---

### 5. What should you do if you stumble over your words while practicing on Slide 4 in "Rehearse Timings"?
A. You must restart the entire presentation from Slide 1
B. Click the "Repeat" button on the floating recording toolbar to reset the timer for that specific slide only and try again
C. Delete Slide 4
D. Exit PowerPoint immediately
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The "Repeat" button on the rehearsal toolbar pauses the cumulative clock and restarts the timer exclusively for the active slide.
