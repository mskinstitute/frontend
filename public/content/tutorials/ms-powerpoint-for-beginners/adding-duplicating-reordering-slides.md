# Adding, Duplicating, Deleting & Reordering Slides

Structuring a presentation deck requires agile slide management. Knowing the most efficient methods to insert, clone, rearrange, and eliminate slides accelerates your authoring process.

---

## Adding New Slides

There are multiple ways to insert a new slide into your deck:
- **Ribbon Button**: On the **Home** or **Insert** tab, click the top half of the **New Slide** button to insert a slide with the default layout, or click the bottom half (the arrow) to open the layout picker.
- **Keyboard Shortcut**: Press **`Ctrl + M`** to immediately insert a new slide matching the current layout.
- **Enter Key Method**: Click between any two slide thumbnails in the left pane (a horizontal insertion line will appear) and press **Enter**.
- **Reusing Slides**: Click **Home > New Slide > Reuse Slides**. A side pane opens allowing you to browse any existing PowerPoint file on your PC or OneDrive. You can preview individual slides and insert them into your current deck with a single click. Enable the checkbox **Keep source formatting** if you want to preserve the original visual styling.

---

## Duplicating Slides

Duplication is ideal when you have created a complex layout (such as a multi-step timeline or data dashboard) and want to replicate the structure for subsequent points with new content:
- **Keyboard Shortcut**: Select the slide thumbnail on the left and press **`Ctrl + D`**.
- **Right-Click Method**: Right-click the slide thumbnail and select **Duplicate Slide**.
- **Drag-to-Duplicate**: Hold the **Ctrl** key, click on a slide thumbnail, and drag it down. Release the mouse button to drop an exact duplicate.

---

## Reordering Slides

Slide flow dictates audience comprehension:
- **In Normal View**: Click and hold a slide thumbnail in the left pane, drag it up or down to its new position, and release when the horizontal indicator bar reaches the desired target.
- **In Slide Sorter View**: Click **View > Slide Sorter**. The grid display allows you to drag slides across rows and columns quickly.
- **Moving Slide Blocks**: Click the first slide, hold **Shift**, click the last slide in a sequence, and drag the entire block simultaneously.

---

## Deleting and Hiding Slides

- **Deleting**: Select one or more slide thumbnails and press **Delete** or **Backspace**.
- **Undo Accidental Deletion**: Immediately press **`Ctrl + Z`** to restore the deleted slide with all content, animations, and transitions intact.
- **Hiding vs. Deleting**: If you want to keep supplementary backup data slides (e.g., detailed financial calculations for Q&A) without showing them during your main pitch, right-click the thumbnail and choose **Hide Slide**. The slide stays safely in your deck but will not appear during the presentation.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which keyboard shortcut immediately duplicates the currently selected slide in the left thumbnail pane?
A. Ctrl + C followed by Ctrl + V
B. Ctrl + D
C. Ctrl + Shift + D
D. Alt + D
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Ctrl + D duplicates the selected object or slide in a single step without needing to copy and paste.

---

### 2. How can you insert slides from a completely different presentation file without opening it in a separate window?
A. Use File > Import > Convert
B. Use Home > New Slide > Reuse Slides
C. Press Ctrl + Alt + R
D. Drag the file from the Windows desktop onto the status bar
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The "Reuse Slides" panel allows users to browse other presentation files, preview their slides, and selectively insert them into the active deck.

---

### 3. What happens when you press the Enter key while the insertion line between two slide thumbnails in the left pane is active?
A. The selected slide is deleted
B. A new slide is inserted at that exact position
C. The presentation begins playing in full screen
D. The spelling checker is launched
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Pressing Enter when focused in the thumbnail list inserts a new slide immediately following the active slide.

---

### 4. If you have 5 backup slides containing appendix data that you only want to view if an audience member asks a specific question, what is the best practice?
A. Delete them before presenting and re-type them later
B. Hide the slides by right-clicking them and selecting "Hide Slide"
C. Change their font color to white
D. Move them to Slide 1
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Hiding slides prevents them from appearing in normal sequential slide show playback while keeping them accessible via hyperlink or slide picker if needed during Q&A.

---

### 5. What key do you hold down while clicking multiple slide thumbnails to select non-contiguous slides scattered across a deck?
A. Shift
B. Ctrl
C. Alt
D. Tab
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Holding Ctrl allows multi-selecting specific non-adjacent slides, whereas Shift selects a contiguous range from first to last.
