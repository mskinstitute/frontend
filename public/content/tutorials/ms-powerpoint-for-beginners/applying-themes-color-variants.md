# Applying Themes, Color Variants & Font Schemes

Design consistency is the hallmark of professional decks. PowerPoint's **Themes** architecture controls universal palettes, typographic pairings, and background styles across an entire presentation with a single click, preventing mismatched fonts and discordant colors.

![Themes, Variants & Format Background](/images/tutorials/ms-powerpoint/design-themes-background.svg)

---

## The Three Components of an Office Theme

An **Office Theme** is a unified design package composed of three interdependent layers:
1. **Theme Colors**: A coordinated palette of 12 colors:
   - 4 Text and Background colors (Light 1, Light 2, Dark 1, Dark 2)
   - 6 Accent colors (used for charts, shape fills, callouts, and emphasis)
   - 2 Hyperlink colors (standard link and followed link)
2. **Theme Fonts**: A designated pair consisting of a **Heading Font** (used for titles) and a **Body Font** (used for bullet points and tables).
3. **Theme Effects**: Subtle shadow, line, and reflection styles applied to 3D shapes and SmartArt diagrams.

---

## Applying & Switching Themes

Browse and apply themes on the **Design** tab:
1. Click the downward scroll arrow on the **Themes** gallery to preview dozens of built-in templates.
2. Hovering your mouse over any theme displays a real-time live preview across your slides.
3. Click any theme to apply it across all slides in the deck.
4. **Selective Application**: If you want a theme applied to only a specific section or slide, right-click the theme thumbnail in the gallery and choose **Apply to Selected Slides**.

---

## Exploring Color Variants & Custom Palettes

Directly next to the Themes gallery is the **Variants** group:
- **Variant Cards**: Four pre-designed color and texture alternates of the active theme.
- **Customizing Colors**: Click the downward chevron in the Variants box > **Colors**:
  - Choose from dozens of historical Office palettes (e.g., *Office 2013-2022*, *Apex*, *Metro*, *Teal*, *Warm*).
  - Click **Customize Colors** at the bottom to define your organization's exact RGB or Hex color codes for Accent 1 through Accent 6. When you update Accent 1, every chart, icon, and colored box using Accent 1 updates synchronously!
- **Customizing Fonts**: Go to **Variants > Fonts > Customize Fonts**. Set your company's official corporate Heading and Body fonts (e.g., Segoe UI Semibold and Segoe UI). Name and save the font pairing for reuse across all future presentations.

---

## Theme Colors vs. Standard Colors

When picking colors from the color palette dropdown:
- **Theme Colors (Top Grid)**: Colors connected to the active theme palette. If you switch themes later, elements using these colors automatically update to match the new theme.
- **Standard Colors (Bottom Row)**: Fixed universal colors (Dark Red, Red, Orange, Yellow, Light Green, Green, Light Blue, Blue, Dark Blue, Purple). These colors **never** change when switching themes.
- **EyeDropper Tool**: Pick any exact color from a company logo, photograph, or website on your screen to match branding flawlessly.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What three core elements comprise a complete Microsoft Office Theme?
A. Margin widths, print orientation, and paper size
B. Theme Colors, Theme Fonts, and Theme Effects
C. Transitions, Animations, and Audio tracks
D. Macros, VBA code, and Add-ins
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** An Office Theme is defined by a 12-color Theme Colors palette, a 2-font Theme Fonts pairing (Heading & Body), and shape Theme Effects.

---

### 2. How can you apply a new theme to only two specific slides without altering the rest of your presentation deck?
A. Themes can only ever be applied to all slides simultaneously
B. Select the two slides, right-click the desired theme in the Design gallery, and select "Apply to Selected Slides"
C. Copy the slides into Microsoft Paint
D. Turn off AutoSave
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Right-clicking any theme thumbnail provides the option "Apply to Selected Slides", enabling mixed-theme presentations or distinct section cover styling.

---

### 3. What is the benefit of using "Theme Colors" instead of "Standard Colors" when coloring shapes and text?
A. Theme colors make files load faster
B. If you change the presentation theme or color variant later, all elements using theme colors update automatically to match
C. Theme colors are strictly black and white
D. Standard colors are deleted when saving
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Theme colors are dynamically linked to the presentation palette; changing theme variants automatically recalculates all linked shape and text colors.

---

### 4. Which tool in the color picker allows you to sample and match an exact color from an imported company logo on your slide?
A. Format Painter
B. Eyedropper
C. Color Replacement Tool
D. Magic Wand
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The Eyedropper tool samples the exact RGB value of any pixel on your canvas and applies it directly to the active shape, outline, or font.

---

### 5. How many accent colors are defined within every standard Office Theme color palette?
A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 12
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Every Office theme defines 6 distinct Accent colors (Accent 1 through Accent 6) utilized sequentially by charts, SmartArt, and shapes.
