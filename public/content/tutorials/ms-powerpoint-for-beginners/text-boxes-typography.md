# Text Boxes, Typography, Fonts & Text Alignment

Typography is the backbone of presentation design. Poor font choices, cramped text boxes, and low-contrast colors cause cognitive overload, while clean typographic hierarchy and deliberate alignment deliver message clarity and professional authority.

## Working with Text Boxes

While native slide layouts provide pre-configured placeholders, custom callouts, badges, and captions often require custom **Text Boxes**:
1. Go to **Insert > Text Box** (or **Home > Drawing > Text Box**).
2. **Fixed-Width Text Box**: Click and drag on the slide to create a box with a specific width. Text typed inside will automatically wrap at the right margin.
3. **Auto-Expanding Text Box**: Single-click on the slide without dragging. The text box will expand horizontally on a single line as you type until you press Enter.

### Formatting Text Box Properties
Right-click any text box border and choose **Format Shape > Text Options**:
- **Text autofit options**:
  - *Do not Autofit* (recommended for strict design consistency)
  - *Shrink text on overflow* (reduces font size automatically if text exceeds box limits)
  - *Resize shape to fit text*
- **Internal Margins**: Adjust Left, Right, Top, and Bottom margins to control padding between text and borders.
- **Vertical Alignment**: Align text to the Top, Middle, or Bottom of the box container.

## Typography Best Practices in Presentations

Professional presentation designers adhere to strict typographic standards:

### 1. The 10/20/30 Rule for Fonts
Coined by venture capitalist Guy Kawasaki: presentations should never use font sizes smaller than **28 pt to 30 pt** for body text and **36 pt to 44 pt** for slide titles. If an audience member seated in the back row cannot read your slide, the text is too small.

### 2. Font Pairing (Header + Body)
Limit each presentation to a maximum of **two font families**:
- **Modern Clean**: Montserrat / Poppins (Title) paired with Open Sans / Calibri (Body)
- **Corporate Executive**: Segoe UI Semibold (Title) paired with Segoe UI (Body)
- **Traditional Elegant**: Georgia / Garamond (Title) paired with Calibri (Body)

### 3. Contrast & Legibility
- Dark backgrounds require crisp light text (white, pale cream, or light pastel).
- Light backgrounds require deep charcoal or dark navy text (pure black `#000000` can sometimes appear overly harsh; dark slate `#1E293B` provides softer readability).

## Paragraph Alignment & Columns

On the **Home** tab in the **Paragraph** group:
- **Horizontal Alignment**:
  - **Align Left (`Ctrl + L`)**: The standard for presentation text; predictable left edge aids natural eye tracking.
  - **Center (`Ctrl + E`)**: Ideal for slide titles, quotes, and single-line KPI statistics. Avoid for multi-line body paragraphs.
  - **Align Right (`Ctrl + R`)**: Used for captions, data dates, and side callouts.
  - **Justify (`Ctrl + J`)**: Avoid in presentations; uneven spacing between words creates distracting "rivers of white space".
- **Multi-Column Text**: Click **Add or Remove Columns** to divide a single text box into 2 or 3 even columns without creating separate boxes.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is the recommended minimum font size for body text on presentation slides to ensure readability from the back of a room?
A. 10 pt to 12 pt
B. 14 pt to 16 pt
C. 28 pt to 32 pt
D. 72 pt
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Standard presentation design guidelines (such as the 10/20/30 rule) recommend 28 pt to 32 pt minimum for body text so slides remain legible at a distance.

---

### 2. Which keyboard shortcut centers the text within the active text box or placeholder?
A. Ctrl + C
B. Ctrl + E
C. Ctrl + M
D. Ctrl + Shift + C
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Ctrl + E centers text, while Ctrl + L aligns left and Ctrl + R aligns right (Ctrl + C is reserved for Copy).

---

### 3. What happens if you single-click on the slide canvas with the Text Box tool selected rather than clicking and dragging a rectangle?
A. PowerPoint inserts a table
B. An auto-expanding text box is created that grows horizontally as you type without word wrapping
C. The tool is cancelled
D. The slide background is deleted
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A single click creates an auto-fitting text box that expands horizontally on a single line until Enter is pressed, whereas dragging creates a box with fixed wrapping boundaries.

---

### 4. How can you divide text inside a single text box into two neat vertical columns without creating two separate text boxes?
A. Press the Tab key 10 times
B. Use the "Add or Remove Columns" button in the Paragraph group on the Home tab
C. Draw a line through the center of the text box
D. Change the font to Courier New
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The "Add or Remove Columns" command on the Home tab divides any text box or placeholder into 2, 3, or more balanced vertical columns.

---

### 5. Why do presentation design experts generally advise against using Justified alignment (`Ctrl + J`) in presentation slides?
A. PowerPoint does not support justified text
B. Justified text creates irregular gaps between words ("rivers of white space") that hurt readability at large font sizes
C. Justified text increases file size
D. Justified text cannot be printed
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Large slide typography causes justified alignment to space words unevenly, resulting in visual gaps that distract the audience and hinder eye tracking.
