# Inserting Audio, Video & Screen Recording in Slides

Dynamic multimedia captures attention, demonstrates software, and preserves spoken narration. PowerPoint provides comprehensive support for embedded video files, online video streaming, background music tracks, and an integrated desktop screen recorder.

---

## Inserting & Formatting Video Clips

### Supported Video Formats
PowerPoint performs best with **.mp4 files encoded with H.264 video and AAC audio**. It also supports `.wmv`, `.mov`, and `.avi`.

### Embedding Local Video
1. Go to **Insert > Video > This Device**.
2. Select your video file and click **Insert**.
3. The video appears on your slide with an interactive playback scrubber bar underneath.

### The Video Format & Playback Tabs
Selecting an embedded video activates two contextual tabs:
- **Video Format**: Apply stylish frames, rounded borders, color corrections, and set a custom **Poster Frame** (the thumbnail image displayed before the video plays).
- **Playback Tab**:
  - **Start**: Choose whether playback begins **In Click Sequence**, **Automatically** upon navigating to the slide, or **When Clicked On**.
  - **Trim Video**: Set custom Start Time and End Time markers to cut unwanted footage without external software.
  - **Fade Duration**: Add smooth Fade In and Fade Out audio/video transitions (e.g., 0.5 seconds).
  - **Play Full Screen**: Video automatically expands to cover the entire display during playback.
  - **Loop until Stopped**: Restarts continuously until the presenter advances to the next slide.

---

## Adding Audio Narration & Background Music

Go to **Insert > Audio**:
- **Audio on My PC**: Insert background music (`.mp3` or `.wav`).
- **Record Audio**: Use your microphone to record spoken commentary directly onto the slide.

### Background Music Playback Across All Slides
To play ambient background music during an entire slide presentation:
1. Insert the audio file on Slide 1.
2. Go to the **Playback** tab.
3. Click the single preset button: **Play in Background**.
4. PowerPoint automatically configures:
   - *Start: Automatically*
   - *Play Across Slides* (music does not cut off when advancing slides)
   - *Loop until Stopped*
   - *Hide During Show* (makes the audio speaker icon invisible to the audience)

---

## Using the Built-In Screen Recorder

PowerPoint includes a full-featured screen recorder perfect for software tutorials, website demos, and technical walkthroughs:
1. Open the window or application you want to record.
2. In PowerPoint, go to **Insert > Screen Recording**.
3. The screen dims and a floating recording control dock appears at the top.
4. Click **Select Area** (or press **`Windows Key + Shift + A`**) and drag a marquee over the portion of your desktop to capture.
5. Ensure **Audio** and **Record Pointer** are toggled on as needed.
6. Click **Record** (or press **`Windows Key + Shift + R`**). A 3-second countdown initiates.
7. Perform your software demonstration.
8. When finished, press **`Windows Key + Shift + Q`** to stop recording.
9. The recorded video is immediately inserted onto your PowerPoint slide, ready for playback, trimming, and compression!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which video file format and codec is recommended by Microsoft for optimal performance and compatibility in modern PowerPoint?
A. .flv with Flash
B. .mp4 encoded with H.264 video and AAC audio
C. .avi uncompressed
D. .mov with Apple ProRes 4444
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio is the cross-platform standard recommended for maximum compatibility and smooth playback in PowerPoint.

---

### 2. Which single button on the Audio Playback tab configures audio to loop seamlessly across all slides while hiding the speaker icon from the audience?
A. Loop Track
B. Play in Background
C. Ambient Mode
D. Kiosk Playback
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The "Play in Background" preset automatically activates "Play Across Slides", "Loop until Stopped", and "Hide During Show" in one click.

---

### 3. Which keyboard shortcut stops an active PowerPoint Screen Recording session?
A. Windows Key + Shift + Q
B. Ctrl + Alt + Delete
C. Esc
D. Alt + F4
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Pressing Windows Key + Shift + Q immediately halts the screen recorder and places the captured video file onto your active slide.

---

### 4. What is a "Poster Frame" in PowerPoint video formatting?
A. A wooden frame drawn around the video border
B. The static thumbnail image displayed on the slide before the video starts playing
C. A printed movie poster
D. A watermark that cannot be removed
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Poster Frame is the preview cover image displayed on a slide before the viewer clicks play, selectable from video frames or custom image files.

---

### 5. What tool on the Video Playback tab allows presenters to cut out the beginning or end of a video clip directly inside PowerPoint?
A. Video Splitter
B. Trim Video
C. Segment Cutter
D. Remove Intro
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The "Trim Video" dialog lets users drag green start and red end markers to trim video footage without third-party editing tools.
