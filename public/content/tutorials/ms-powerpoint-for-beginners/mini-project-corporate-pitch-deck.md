# Capstone Project 1: Designing an Executive Business Pitch Deck

Putting theory into practice is the definitive mark of mastery. In this hands-on capstone project, you will apply typography, layouts, custom Slide Master design, iconography, charts, and cinematic Morph transitions to build an executive 10-slide startup fundraising pitch deck.

![Capstone Pitch Deck Template](/images/tutorials/ms-powerpoint/pitch-deck-template.svg)

---

## Project Specifications & Architecture

- **Project Goal**: Build an investor-ready 10-slide pitch deck for a fictional technology company: *"CloudSync AI - Automated Enterprise Workspace"*.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Background: Deep Slate Navy (`#0B0F19`)
  - Surface Card Containers: Charcoal (`#111827`) with subtle border (`#374151`)
  - Accent / Brand Color: Electric Orange (`#D24726`)
  - Success Metric Color: Emerald Green (`#10B981`)
  - Text: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for headings, Muted Silver (`#9CA3AF`) for body copy
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - Headings: *Montserrat ExtraBold* or *Segoe UI Semibold* (36 pt - 44 pt)
  - Body: *Segoe UI* or *Open Sans* (16 pt - 20 pt)

---

## The 10-Slide Standard Pitch Structure

Follow the venture-standard slide sequence:

1. **Title Slide**: Strong product tagline, company logo, presenter name, date, and confidentiality badge.
2. **The Problem**: 3 structured cards highlighting current enterprise pain points (wasted hours, fragmented tools, high churn).
3. **The Solution**: Hero product diagram demonstrating how CloudSync AI solves the pain points in one platform.
4. **Market Opportunity (TAM/SAM/SOM)**: 3 concentric circles or visual callout cards displaying Total Addressable Market ($48B), Serviceable Addressable Market ($14B), and Obtainable Market ($2.8B).
5. **Product Features**: 4 icon cards using SVG icons and short benefit-driven headlines.
6. **Business Model**: Clean 3-tier pricing table (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) highlighting the middle tier.
7. **Market Traction & Revenue**: A native clustered column or line chart demonstrating 180% year-over-year MRR growth.
8. **Competitive Advantage**: A 2x2 matrix or comparison checkmark table positioning CloudSync AI against legacy competitors.
9. **The Team**: 3 circular headshot picture placeholders (created via Slide Master or Crop to Shape) with executive bios and past credentials.
10. **The Investment Ask & Vision**: Clear dollar ask ($3.5M Seed Round), specific fund allocation pie/doughnut chart, contact email, and call-to-action button.

## Step-by-Step Build Checklist

### Step 1: Slide Master & Branding Setup
1. Go to **View > Slide Master**.
2. Select the top parent master. Set the background to Solid Fill (`#0B0F19`).
3. Set Heading font to *Segoe UI Semibold* and Body font to *Segoe UI*.
4. Insert a subtle footer disclaimer: `CloudSync AI Inc. | Confidential Investor Presentation`.
5. Return to Normal View.

### Step 2: Designing the 3-Card Problem Slide
1. Insert a new slide with **Title Only** layout.
2. Title: `The Problem & Market Opportunity`.
3. Draw 3 rounded rectangles across the canvas.
4. Use **Shape Format > Align > Distribute Horizontally** to ensure mathematical spacing.
5. Insert red/warning icons on each card with a bold metric (e.g., *83% of remote teams struggle with alignment*).

### Step 3: Adding the Cinematic Morph Transition
1. Select Slide 2 (The Problem) and press **`Ctrl + D`** to duplicate it as Slide 3 (The Solution).
2. On Slide 3, change the title to `Our Solution: Unified Intelligent Automation`.
3. Shrink the problem cards and shift them to the side, then expand the center card to 250% size, coloring it in brand orange (`#D24726`).
4. Apply the **Morph transition** to Slide 3 with **Duration: 01.50s**.
5. Test with **`Shift + F5`**: watch the problem cards glide and transform dynamically into the solution showcase!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. In executive pitch deck design, what does the market sizing acronym "TAM" stand for?
A. Technical Asset Management
B. Total Addressable Market
C. Temporary Annual Margin
D. Targeted Advertising Method
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** TAM stands for Total Addressable Market, representing the overall revenue opportunity available if a product achieved 100% market share.

---

### 2. What presentation design practice ensures that 3 problem cards on a slide have perfectly balanced gaps between them?
A. Eye-balling the alignment by dragging slowly
B. Selecting all 3 shapes and clicking Shape Format > Align > Distribute Horizontally
C. Grouping the cards into a table
D. Setting slide size to 4:3
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** "Distribute Horizontally" mathematically equalizes spacing between all selected objects across the horizontal axis.

---

### 3. Why is dark mode styling (`#0B0F19` background with white text) frequently chosen for modern technology pitch decks?
A. It consumes less disk space
B. It provides a sleek, modern visual aesthetic that makes accent colors and illuminated metric charts pop vividly
C. Text cannot be copied from dark slides
D. Dark slides cannot be edited
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Deep dark backgrounds create high contrast, allowing bright accent colors and glowing charts to command visual prominence on modern LED displays.

---

### 4. What is the recommended slide count range for an initial investor presentation pitch deck?
A. 1 to 2 slides
B. 10 to 12 slides
C. 50 to 75 slides
D. Exactly 100 slides
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The venture capital standard (such as the 10/20/30 rule) recommends 10 to 12 slides for a concise, compelling executive pitch deck.

---

### 5. Which transition applied between a "Problem" slide and a duplicated "Solution" slide creates a smooth, cinematic shift in object sizes and colors?
A. Random Bars
B. Morph
C. Split
D. Honeycomb
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Morph detects shared elements between consecutive slides, smoothly animating their size, position, and color changes.
