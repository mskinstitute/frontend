# Hyperlinks, Action Buttons & Slide Zoom Navigation

Traditional presentations force you into a rigid, sequential "Slide 1 to Slide 2 to Slide 3" linear delivery. Modern presentations leverage **Hyperlinks**, **Action Buttons**, and **Slide Zoom** to create interactive, non-linear dashboards, clickable self-service kiosks, and dynamic branching presentations.

![Slide Zoom & Interactive Navigation](/images/tutorials/ms-powerpoint/hyperlinks-and-zoom.svg)

---

## Creating Hyperlinks (`Ctrl + K`)

Hyperlinks connect slide elements to external resources or other slides within your deck:
1. Select any text, shape, icon, or picture.
2. Go to **Insert > Link** (or press **`Ctrl + K`**).
3. In the **Insert Hyperlink** dialog, choose from 4 target destinations:
   - **Existing File or Web Page**: Opens an external website URL (e.g., your company portal or live dashboard) or opens a separate file (e.g., an Excel model or PDF contract).
   - **Place in This Document**: Select a specific slide (e.g., `Slide 14 - Financial Model`) or jump to predefined targets (*First Slide*, *Last Slide*, *Next Slide*, *Previous Slide*).
   - **Create New Document**: Generates a new file when clicked.
   - **E-mail Address**: Opens the user's default email client pre-addressed to a recipient with a designated subject line.

---

## Interactive Action Buttons

Action Buttons are ready-to-use vector shapes configured with pre-programmed interactive behaviors:
1. Go to **Insert > Shapes**.
2. Scroll to the very bottom category: **Action Buttons**.
3. Select an icon shape:
   - *Home* (house icon)
   - *Help* (question mark)
   - *Information* (i icon)
   - *Back or Previous / Forward or Next*
   - *Beginning / End*
   - *Return* (jumps back to the most recently viewed slide)
   - *Sound*
4. Click and drag on your slide to place the button.
5. The **Action Settings** dialog immediately appears automatically!
6. Configure actions for two distinct user states:
   - **Mouse Click**: Action triggers when clicked.
   - **Mouse Over**: Action triggers when the presenter simply hovers their cursor over the button without clicking (great for interactive map pins or tooltips).
7. Available actions include: *Hyperlink to*, *Run program*, *Play sound*, or *Highlight click*.

## The Power of PowerPoint Zoom Navigation

Introduced in Microsoft 365, the **Zoom** engine creates seamless Prezi-style non-linear visual zooming:
- **Summary Zoom**: Generates a landing dashboard containing clickable mini-previews of each section. During the show, clicking Section 2 zooms deep into that slide. When Section 2 finishes, PowerPoint smoothly zooms back out to the master agenda dashboard!
- **Section Zoom**: Drag a section header directly from the thumbnail pane onto any slide canvas to create an instant interactive zoom tile.
- **Slide Zoom**: Drag any individual slide thumbnail from the left pane directly onto your active slide canvas.
- **Zoom Tools Tab**: When a zoom tile is selected:
  - **Return to Zoom**: Automatically returns to the menu slide once the target slide completes.
  - **Zoom Transition**: Toggles smooth 3D camera zooming.
  - **Duration**: Speed of the zoom camera glide.
  - **Change Image**: Replace the default slide screenshot tile with a sleek corporate icon or customized card illustration.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What universal keyboard shortcut opens the "Insert Hyperlink" dialog box for any selected text or shape?
A. Ctrl + H
B. Ctrl + K
C. Ctrl + L
D. Alt + H
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Ctrl + K is the universal shortcut in Microsoft Office to create or edit hyperlinks to websites, local files, or slides.

---

### 2. Which category at the bottom of the Insert > Shapes gallery features pre-configured interactive buttons for Home, Sound, and Navigation?
A. Callouts
B. Flowchart
C. Action Buttons
D. Block Arrows
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The "Action Buttons" shape category provides ready-made interactive buttons that immediately open the Action Settings dialog upon placement.

---

### 3. What interactive behavior can be assigned to an Action Button that fires without the presenter clicking the mouse button?
A. Mouse Over (Hover)
B. Voice Activation
C. Double Click
D. Right Click
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The "Mouse Over" tab in Action Settings triggers commands (such as playing a sound or jumping to a slide) simply by hovering the cursor over the shape.

---

### 4. In PowerPoint Slide Zoom, what does the "Return to Zoom" option accomplish?
A. It resets the slide zoom to 100% in Normal View
B. It automatically zooms the camera back to the summary menu slide after the linked section or slide finishes playing
C. It exits the slide show
D. It deletes the zoom tile
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** "Return to Zoom" instructs PowerPoint to return the audience smoothly back to the overview dashboard slide after presenting the zoomed content.

---

### 5. How can you quickly create an interactive Slide Zoom tile on your active slide canvas without navigating the Ribbon?
A. Drag a slide thumbnail from the left thumbnail pane directly onto your active slide canvas
B. Press Ctrl + Alt + Z
C. Right-click the status bar
D. Export the slide to an image and paste it
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Simply dragging any slide thumbnail from the left pane and dropping it onto the active canvas automatically instantiates a functional Slide Zoom tile.
