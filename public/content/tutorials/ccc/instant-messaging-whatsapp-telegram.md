# Instant Messaging: WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger (इंस्टेंट मैसेजिंग)

**Instant Messaging (IM)** refers to digital communication services that facilitate real-time, instantaneous text transmission, voice calling, video chatting, and file sharing over the internet.

Unlike traditional SMS (Short Message Service), which charges per text over cellular telecom networks, instant messaging uses internet data and supports rich multimedia.

---

## 1. Leading Instant Messaging Apps in India

### A. WhatsApp (Meta)
- The most ubiquitous communication app in India (over 500 million active users).
- **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE):** All messages, calls, photos, and voice notes are encrypted so that **only you and the person you are communicating with can read them**—not even WhatsApp or hackers can intercept them!
- Features: Groups (up to 1,024 members), Communities, Channels, Voice/Video Calls, UPI Payments (**WhatsApp Pay**).

### B. Telegram
- Cloud-based instant messaging platform founded by Pavel Durov.
- **Supergroups & Channels:** Channels can have an **unlimited number of subscribers** for broadcasting study notes and news.
- **Large File Sharing:** Allows sending single files up to **`2 GB`** in size!
- Cloud storage: Messages and media are saved in Telegram's cloud, freeing up your mobile phone's internal storage.

### C. Signal Private Messenger
- Open-source, non-profit messaging application renowned as the gold standard for cryptographic privacy and zero user data collection.

---

## 2. Groups vs Channels (ग्रुप बनाम चैनल)

- **Group (ग्रुप):** An interactive multi-user chat room where **all members can talk, text, and share** files with one another (e.g. a college friend circle).
- **Channel (चैनल):** A one-way broadcast tool where **only administrators can post updates**, and members can only read and download content (e.g. MSK Institute Study Notes Channel).

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What does "End-to-End Encryption" (E2EE) in WhatsApp signify?
A. Messages are saved in government archives
B. Only the sender and recipient can read the messages; third parties and service providers cannot decrypt them
C. Messages expire after 10 seconds
D. Messages can only be sent during business hours
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** End-to-End Encryption ensures that data is scrambled at the sender's device and decrypted only at the recipient's endpoint, preventing anyone in transit from reading the content.

---

### 2. What is the maximum single file size that can be shared on Telegram?
A. 25 MB
B. 100 MB
C. 2 GB
D. 10 GB
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Telegram supports cloud-based sharing of individual media, documents, and archives up to 2 Gigabytes (2 GB) in size for free users.

---

### 3. Which communication model describes a Telegram "Channel"?
A. Two-way video conference
B. One-way broadcast communication from administrators to unlimited subscribers
C. Multi-player gaming room
D. Private peer-to-peer file transfer
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Channel is a broadcast mechanism where administrators publish information to an unlimited audience of subscribers who receive updates passively.

---

### 4. Which messaging application is widely acclaimed by security experts as a non-profit, open-source standard for personal data privacy?
A. Signal
B. WeChat
C. Yahoo Messenger
D. BBM
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Signal is an open-source, non-profit encrypted messaging app that does not collect metadata, trackers, or commercial user logs.

---

### 5. What protocol did traditional cellular phone text messages use prior to the advent of internet-based instant messaging?
A. HTTP
B. SMS (Short Message Service)
C. FTP
D. SMTP
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** SMS (Short Message Service) was the standard cellular telephone protocol for sending short 160-character plain text messages over mobile telephone carrier channels.
