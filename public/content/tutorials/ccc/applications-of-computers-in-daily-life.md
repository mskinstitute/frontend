# Applications of Computers in Daily Life (दैनिक जीवन में कंप्यूटर के उपयोग)

Computers have transformed every sector of modern society. From booking train tickets on Indian Railways to attending online classes in UP villages, computer literacy (कंप्यूटर साक्षरता) has become a fundamental skill for employment and personal growth.

## 1. Major Sectors Using Computers

### A. Education & Learning (शिक्षा के क्षेत्र में)
- **Smart Classrooms:** Interactive whiteboards and digital projectors help students understand complex science and math diagrams.
- **E-Learning Platforms:** Websites like SWAYAM, DIKSHA, YouTube, and Coursera allow students in remote areas to learn from top professors.
- **Online Examinations:** Government exams like NIELIT CCC, UPSSSC, SSC, and Railway NTPC are conducted as computer-based tests (CBT).
- **Digital Libraries:** Students can access thousands of books and research papers through National Digital Library of India (NDLI).

### B. Banking & Financial Services (बैंकिंग और वित्त)
- **Core Banking Solutions (CBS):** All bank branches are linked through computer networks, allowing you to deposit or withdraw money from anywhere in India.
- **ATMs (Automated Teller Machines):** 24x7 cash withdrawal, balance check, and PIN change without visiting the bank branch.
- **Online & Mobile Banking:** Transferring funds instantly using **NEFT**, **RTGS**, **IMPS**, and **UPI**.

### C. Government & e-Governance (ई-गवर्नेंस और सरकारी सेवाएं)
- **Digital India Portal:** Applying for government documents online without standing in long queues.
- **Land Records (भूलेख - Bhulekh):** In Uttar Pradesh, farmers can check and print their Khasra-Khatauni land records directly from the UP Bhulekh website.
- **Ration Card & Scholarship Portals:** Online scholarship application and Aadhaar-linked Public Distribution System (PDS) ration distribution.
- **e-District UP:** Online issuance of Income Certificate (आय प्रमाण पत्र), Caste Certificate (जाति प्रमाण पत्र), and Domicile Certificate (निवास प्रमाण पत्र).

### D. Healthcare & Medicine (चिकित्सा और स्वास्थ्य)
- **Diagnostic Equipment:** Advanced medical tests like CT Scan, MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging), Ultrasound, and ECG are completely computer-controlled.
- **Patient Monitoring:** ICU monitors continuously track heartbeat, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation.
- **Telemedicine & e-Hospital:** Online OPD appointments (e-Sanjeevani portal) and digital health records under Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM).

### E. Transportation & Railways (यातायात और रेलवे)
- **IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering & Tourism Corporation):** Millions of passenger train tickets, tatkal reservations, and PNR status checks happen daily through computer servers.
- **FASTag:** RFID-based automatic toll collection at national highway toll plazas.
- **Air Traffic Control (ATC):** Radar computers guide airplanes during takeoff, cruising, and landing to prevent collisions.

### F. Defense & Space Research (रक्षा और अंतरिक्ष अनुसंधान)
- **ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation):** Chandrayaan, Mangalyaan, and Aditya-L1 missions rely on supercomputers to calculate precise satellite trajectories and orbital maneuvers.
- **Guided Missile Systems (DRDO):** Radar tracking, drone control, and defense communication networks.

## 2. Benefits of Computers (कंप्यूटर के मुख्य लाभ)

1. **High Speed (अत्यधिक गति):** Performs millions of calculations in a fraction of a second (measured in MHz / GHz).
2. **Accuracy (सटीकता):** Computers never make calculation mistakes on their own. Errors occur only if incorrect data is supplied by the user (Principle of **GIGO: Garbage In, Garbage Out**).
3. **Diligence (निरंतर कार्य क्षमता):** A computer does not get tired or bored. It can work 24 hours a day with the exact same speed and accuracy.
4. **Storage Capacity (विशाल भंडारण क्षमता):** Stores billions of files, images, videos, and books in a small physical drive.
5. **Versatility (विविधता):** Can play music, prepare balance sheets, edit photos, and forecast weather all on the same machine.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What does the computer principle acronym "GIGO" stand for?
A. Good In, Good Out
B. Garbage In, Garbage Out
C. General Input, General Output
D. Global Internet, Global Operation
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** GIGO stands for "Garbage In, Garbage Out". It means that if inaccurate or faulty data is fed into a computer, the resulting output will also be inaccurate, as computers process data strictly based on instructions provided.

---

### 2. Which portal is used in Uttar Pradesh to verify and download digitized land records (Khasra/Khatauni)?
A. UP Bhulekh
B. UP Seva
C. UP Kisan
D. UP Parivahan
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** UP Bhulekh (upbhulekh.gov.in) is the official land records portal of the Government of Uttar Pradesh where citizens can view, verify, and print their land ownership details online.

---

### 3. Which automated system enables automatic toll tax deduction on Indian National Highways without stopping at toll booths?
A. GPS Tag
B. FASTag
C. AutoToll
D. BharatPay
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** FASTag uses Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology linked to a prepaid wallet or bank account to automatically deduct toll payments while the vehicle is in motion.

---

### 4. What is the primary characteristic that distinguishes computers from human workers?
A. Computers possess emotional intelligence
B. Computers suffer from fatigue and boredom after long working hours
C. Computers possess diligence and work tirelessly without losing speed or accuracy
D. Computers make self-initiated decisions without instructions
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** "Diligence" is a key computer attribute meaning it never experiences physical tiredness, boredom, or loss of concentration, performing repetitive tasks continuously with 100% precision.

---

### 5. Online OPD appointments and government hospital services in India can be accessed through which national portal?
A. e-Hospital (eSanjeevani)
B. IRCTC
C. DigiLocker
D. UMANG Rail
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** e-Hospital and eSanjeevani portals under the Ministry of Health allow citizens to book outpatient appointments, consult doctors via telemedicine, and access digital lab reports online.
