# Online Bill Payment and Recharge (ऑनलाइन बिल भुगतान और रिचार्ज)

Standing in long queues under the sun at electricity department offices or mobile recharge shops has been replaced by instant, 1-click digital bill payment systems.

---

## 1. What is BBPS? (Bharat Bill Payment System - भारत बिलपे)

The **Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS)** is an integrated, interoperable national bill payment ecosystem conceptualized by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and operated by **NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India)**.

### Services Available on BBPS:
- **Electricity Bills (बिजली का बिल):** UPPCL (Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation) electricity bills.
- **Water & Piped Gas Bills:** Municipal water and Indraprastha / Torrent Gas.
- **Mobile Postpaid & Landline:** Airtel, Jio, BSNL broadband and phone.
- **LPG Gas Cylinder Booking:** Indane, Bharat Gas, HP Gas.
- **FASTag Recharge:** Instant highway toll tag top-up.
- **Municipal Property Taxes:** Nagar Nigam house tax.
- **Loan EMI & Insurance Premiums:** Life insurance (LIC) and vehicle EMIs.

---

## 2. Key Advantages of BBPS for Citizens

1. **Anytime, Anywhere (24x7x365):** Pay utility bills even on Sunday midnight.
2. **Instant Payment Confirmation:** Receive immediate digital receipts with a unique **Transaction ID** and SMS confirmation.
3. **Automated Reminders:** Apps like BHIM, PhonePe, and Google Pay fetch your bill automatically each month and notify you before the due date to avoid late penalty fees!
4. **Standardized Grievance Redressal:** If money is debited but the electricity department doesn't update your bill, BBPS provides automated refund tracking and customer dispute resolution.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which organization operates the Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) under RBI guidelines?
A. SEBI
B. NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India)
C. TRAI
D. BSNL
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) is operated by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) as a centralized bill payment infrastructure.

---

### 2. Which of the following utility bills CANNOT be paid through the BBPS network?
A. Electricity Bill (UPPCL)
B. FASTag Recharge
C. Cash payment to a grocery vendor without digital QR
D. LPG Gas Cylinder Booking
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** BBPS handles formalized digital recurring utility, government, and institutional bills; informal direct cash handoffs cannot be routed through BBPS.

---

### 3. What proof of payment does a user receive upon completing an online bill transaction?
A. A physical stamp paper
B. A digital receipt containing a unique Transaction Reference Number
C. A phone call from the minister
D. No proof is provided
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Digital bill payment systems immediately generate an electronic transaction receipt containing a unique transaction reference ID and timestamp.

---

### 4. What technology allows vehicles to pass through toll plazas without stopping, with charges debited from a prepaid account?
A. Barcode
B. FASTag (RFID)
C. Bluetooth
D. QR Code on windshield
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** FASTag uses Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology affixed to a vehicle's windshield to automatically collect toll deductions from linked accounts.

---

### 5. What is the official state power distribution corporation in Uttar Pradesh whose bills can be paid online?
A. UPPCL (Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Limited)
B. MSEB
C. BSES
D. PSPCL
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** UPPCL (Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Limited) is the state electricity utility responsible for electricity transmission and distribution across Uttar Pradesh.
