# Presentation: Creating a New Presentation and Templates (प्रेजेंटेशन परिचय)

A **Presentation** is a visual communication tool consisting of a sequence of digital pages called **Slides** (स्लाइड्स) designed to display text, graphics, charts, sound, and animations on large screens or projectors.

In the NIELIT CCC exam, questions cover both **LibreOffice Impress** (FOSS) and **Microsoft PowerPoint**.

![Presentation Interface and Slides](/images/tutorials/ccc/libreoffice-impress-slide-layout.svg)

---

## 1. Core Presentation Concepts

1. **Slide (स्लाइड):** A single electronic page in a presentation.
2. **Slide Show (स्लाइड शो):** The full-screen automated or manual projection of slides to an audience in sequential order.
3. **Template (टेम्पलेट):** A pre-designed presentation containing pre-coordinated color schemes, fonts, background graphics, and placeholder layouts.
4. **Slide Pane (स्लाइड पेन):** The vertical panel on the left side of the screen displaying miniature thumbnail images of all slides in the presentation.

---

## 2. Creating a Presentation in LibreOffice Impress

When you launch LibreOffice Impress:
- The **"Select a Template"** dialog appears automatically, allowing you to choose from beautiful pre-built styles (*Inspiration, Focus, Vivid, Blue Curve*).
- You can also choose to start with a clean **Blank Presentation (खाली स्लाइड)**.
- Default presentation filename: **`Untitled 1`**.
- Default file extension: **`.odp`** (OpenDocument Presentation) in Impress; **`.pptx`** in MS PowerPoint.

---

## 3. Slide Aspect Ratios (स्लाइड आकार)

Modern presentations support two standard aspect ratios:
- **16:9 Widescreen (16:9 वाइडस्क्रीन - डिफ़ॉल्ट):** Designed for modern flat-screen LED TVs, monitors, and modern widescreen projectors.
- **4:3 Standard (4:3 पारंपरिक):** The classic square-like ratio used by legacy CRT monitors and older classroom overhead projectors.

---

## 4. Inserting New Slides and Duplicating

- **Keyboard Shortcut to Insert a New Slide (CCC Super Question):**
  - **`Ctrl + M`** (Both in LibreOffice Impress and MS PowerPoint!).
  - *(Do not confuse with `Ctrl + N`, which creates an entirely new presentation file!).*
- **Duplicate Slide:** Creates an exact clone of the active slide.
- **Delete Slide:** Select the slide thumbnail in the Slide Pane and press the **`Delete`** key.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is the keyboard shortcut to insert a NEW SLIDE into an existing presentation?
A. `Ctrl + N`
B. `Ctrl + M`
C. `Ctrl + S`
D. `Alt + N`
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** `Ctrl + M` inserts a new slide into the active presentation in both LibreOffice Impress and MS PowerPoint. (`Ctrl + N` creates an entirely new presentation file).

---

### 2. What is the default file extension of a presentation created in LibreOffice Impress?
A. `.odt`
B. `.ods`
C. `.odp`
D. `.pptx`
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** LibreOffice Impress saves presentations in the OpenDocument Presentation format with the `.odp` file extension.

---

### 3. What is an individual electronic page of a presentation called?
A. Sheet
B. Slide
C. Document
D. Frame
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Slide is a single digital page within a presentation containing text, shapes, charts, or multimedia elements.

---

### 4. What is the modern standard widescreen aspect ratio used by presentation slides?
A. 4:3
B. 16:9
C. 1:1
D. 21:9
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** 16:9 (Widescreen) is the modern default aspect ratio for slides, matching standard high-definition television and computer monitor dimensions.

---

### 5. What is the function of a "Template" in presentation software?
A. To detect computer viruses
B. To provide a pre-designed layout, color theme, and formatting structure for quick presentation design
C. To record voice narrations
D. To compress video files
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Template provides a cohesive, pre-designed visual framework containing coordinated font pairings, background styles, and slide layouts, eliminating the need to design slides from scratch.
