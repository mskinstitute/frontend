Artificial Intelligence is no longer just a subject in science fiction movies; it is already integrated into the apps, gadgets, and services we use each day. In Hindi, hum ise kehte hain **"Hamare dainik jeevan mein Artificial Intelligence ke rochhak prayog"**.

From unlocking your smartphone with your face to automatic spam filtering in Gmail, AI silently powers modern conveniences.

### 1. Daily Consumer Applications of AI

Here are the most familiar instances of AI working in everyday life:

| Everyday Application | AI Technology at Work | How It Helps You |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Virtual Voice Assistants** (Google Assistant, Siri, Alexa) | Natural Language Processing (NLP) & Speech-to-Text | Speaks and understands Hindi and English; sets alarms, plays songs, makes calls on voice command. |
| **Smartphone Face Unlock** | Computer Vision & 3D Depth Mapping | Instantly recognizes your facial features even in low light or with spectacles to unlock the phone. |
| **E-Commerce & OTT Recommendations** (Amazon, Flipkart, Netflix, YouTube) | Machine Learning Recommendation Engines | Analyzes your previous watch and purchase history to suggest products and videos you are likely to enjoy. |
| **Smart Typing & Autocorrect** (Gboard, SwiftKey) | Predictive Text Modeling (NLP) | Predicts the next word you want to type, corrects spelling mistakes, and suggests emoji. |
| **Google Maps & Navigation** | Predictive Traffic Analytics | Analyzes live location data from millions of smartphones on the road to calculate the fastest route and traffic jams. |

### 2. AI in Healthcare & Medicine

Artificial Intelligence is revolutionizing modern healthcare, especially in developing regions:

1. **Medical Imaging Diagnostics:** AI vision algorithms analyze X-rays, CT scans, and MRI reports to detect early-stage lung pneumonia, cancer tumors, and bone fractures with accuracy matching senior radiologists.
2. **Diabetic Retinopathy Screening:** Portable AI cameras in rural Indian health clinics photograph a patient's eye retina and detect diabetes-induced blindness in seconds without needing an eye specialist in person.
3. **Telemedicine & Chatbots:** Automated health chatbots triage symptoms, assist patients in booking doctor appointments, and send medication reminders.

### 3. AI in Banking and Financial Fraud Detection

Every second, thousands of transactions pass through banking systems. AI algorithms monitor this massive flow:

- **Credit Card Fraud Detection:** If a debit card usually used in Lucknow is suddenly used to purchase luxury electronics in London 20 minutes later, AI flags the anomaly, freezes the card, and alerts the user via SMS.
- **Algorithmic Trading:** High-frequency computers execute stock market buy and sell orders in milliseconds based on complex mathematical models.
- **Credit Scoring:** Analyzing alternative financial data to provide instant collateral-free microloans to small rural shopkeepers via mobile apps.

---

### 4. Autonomous Vehicles & Smart Transportation

- **Self-Driving Cars (Tesla, Waymo):** Use a combination of cameras, LiDAR sensors, ultrasonic sensors, and deep learning neural networks to detect pedestrians, street signs, lane markers, and other vehicles in real time.
- **Commercial Drones:** AI-piloted drones deliver emergency medicines in remote mountain villages of Uttarakhand and spray fertilizers evenly across crop fields.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which AI subfield enables virtual assistants like Google Assistant, Apple Siri, and Amazon Alexa to understand human spoken language?
A. Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
B. Natural Language Processing (NLP)
C. Computer Aided Design (CAD)
D. Disk Defragmentation
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Natural Language Processing (NLP) enables computers to process, interpret, and generate human spoken and written languages.
---

### 2. How does Google Maps predict traffic congestion and calculate the estimated time of arrival (ETA)?
A. By asking weather forecasters daily
B. By analyzing real-time GPS location and movement speed data aggregated from millions of active smartphones
C. By placing police officers at every road corner
D. By using mechanical speedometers
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Google Maps uses machine learning models to analyze anonymized real-time GPS speed data from millions of mobile users to predict traffic conditions.
---

### 3. Which AI technology allows smartphones to unlock when pointing the front camera at the user's face?
A. Barcode Scanning
B. Computer Vision and Biometric Pattern Recognition
C. Audio Equalizer
D. Bluetooth Pairing
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Computer vision algorithms analyze facial features, depth geometry, and landmark distances to authenticate the registered user.
---

### 4. How does an AI fraud prevention system detect unauthorized debit card transactions?
A. By manually calling every customer for every transaction
B. By identifying anomalous spending patterns, sudden foreign locations, or unusual transaction sizes in real-time
C. By cancelling all credit cards every week
D. By turning off the bank's servers at night
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Machine learning models detect fraud by analyzing anomalies that deviate significantly from a customer's typical purchasing behavior, location, and amounts.
---

### 5. In agriculture, what role do AI-equipped drones play?
A. Playing music to help plants grow faster
B. Monitoring crop health, detecting pest infestations, and spraying fertilizers with high precision
C. Harvesting wheat manually with robotic arms
D. Drilling borewells underground
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Agricultural AI drones survey farmlands using multispectral cameras to identify crop stress and deliver targeted pesticide spraying.
