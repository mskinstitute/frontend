# Transitions, Animations, and Slide Timings (ट्रांजिशन और एनिमेशन)

Visual movement brings excitement to a lecture. However, many students and beginners confuse **Slide Transitions** with **Custom Animations**. 

In the NIELIT CCC examination, questions testing the difference between transitions and animations are asked frequently.

---

## 1. Transitions vs Animations: The Crucial Difference

```text
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Feature               | Slide Transition            | Custom Animation            |
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Where applied?        | Applied to the **ENTIRE SLIDE**| Applied to **INDIVIDUAL OBJECTS**|
|                       | as a whole                  | (text lines, pictures, shapes)|
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| When does it occur?   | Occurs when moving **from one| Occurs **WHILE remaining ON the|
|                       | slide to the next slide**   | active slide**              |
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Examples              | Wipe, Fade, Push, Dissolve, | Fly In, Zoom, Bounce,       |
|                       | Split, Wheel                | Spin, Teeter, Fade Out      |
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
```

## 2. Slide Transitions (स्लाइड ट्रांजिशन)

A **Transition** is the visual motion effect that occurs in the transition phase when one slide exits and the next slide enters the screen during a slide show.

### Configuring Transitions in LibreOffice Impress:
1. In the right-hand Sidebar deck, click the **Slide Transition** icon.
2. Choose from transition effects (*Wipe, Wheel, Dissolve, Fade*).
3. **Modify Transition Properties:**
   - **Duration:** Speed of transition (e.g. 1.0 second).
   - **Sound:** Optional audio chime when the slide changes.
4. **Advance Slide (स्लाइड आगे बढ़ाना):**
   - *On mouse click:* Slide advances only when you click the mouse or press Spacebar.
   - *Automatically after X seconds:* Advances automatically without manual intervention (ideal for automated kiosk displays!).
5. **"Apply Transition to All Slides":** Click this button to enforce a consistent transition across your entire presentation.

## 3. Custom Animations (ऑब्जेक्ट एनिमेशन)

**Animations** control how individual elements (bullet points, photos, titles) appear, emphasize, and disappear on a slide.

Animation effects are divided into four categories:
1. **Entrance (प्रवेश):** How the object appears on the slide (e.g. *Fly In from bottom, Zoom In*).
2. **Emphasis (प्रभाव):** Draws attention to an already visible object (e.g. *Spin, Pulse, Change Color*).
3. **Exit (प्रस्थान):** How an object leaves the slide (e.g. *Fly Out, Fade Away*).
4. **Motion Paths (गति पथ):** Guides an object along a custom geometric trajectory (e.g. a car moving across the screen along a curve).

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is the visual motion effect that occurs when advancing from one slide to another called?
A. Custom Animation
B. Slide Transition
C. Macro
D. Slide Master
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Slide Transition is the visual animation that governs the entrance and exit of an entire slide when moving to the subsequent slide.

---

### 2. What is an animation applied to an individual bullet point or photograph on a slide called?
A. Slide Transition
B. Custom Animation
C. Screen Saver
D. Slide Timing
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Custom Animations are applied to specific individual elements (like text boxes, pictures, or charts) on a slide to control their entrance, emphasis, or exit.

---

### 3. Which category of animation controls how an element initially appears onto the slide?
A. Exit Effect
B. Entrance Effect
C. Motion Path
D. Sound Effect
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Entrance effects dictate how an object makes its initial entrance onto the slide during a presentation (e.g. Fly In, Zoom, Fade).

---

### 4. What feature in the Slide Transition panel allows an exhibition kiosk presentation to cycle through slides without manual clicking?
A. On mouse click
B. Automatically after X seconds
C. Loop Macro
D. Rehearse Timings
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Setting "Advance slide automatically after X seconds" instructs the presentation engine to transition slides automatically after the designated duration expires.

---

### 5. Which of the following is an example of a Slide Transition effect?
A. Bold text
B. Wipe / Dissolve
C. Spell Check
D. Mail Merge
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Wipe, Dissolve, Fade, and Push are standard visual transition effects that bridge two consecutive slides.
