# Spelling Check, Find and Replace, AutoCorrect (वर्तनी जांच और सुधार)

Proofreading long official documents manually is time-consuming and prone to human error. Word processors feature built-in automated linguistic proofing tools to detect grammatical mistakes, replace repeated errors, and autocorrect typos on the fly.

---

## 1. Automated Spelling & Grammar Checking

As you type, the word processor continuously cross-references your words against its internal dictionary:

- **Red Wavy Line (लाल टेढ़ी-मेढ़ी रेखा):** Indicates a **Spelling Mistake** (वर्तनी त्रुटि) or an unknown word (such as an Indian proper name like "Ayodhya" or "Sumit").
  - *To fix:* Right-click the red-underlined word to select the correct spelling, or click **"Add to Dictionary"** so it is recognized permanently.
- **Blue Wavy Line (नीली टेढ़ी-मेढ़ी रेखा):** Indicates a **Grammar or Punctuation Error** (e.g. missing comma, wrong tense, or extra spaces between words).

### Proofing Function Keys (CCC Exam Special):
- **`F7`:** Launches the **Spelling and Grammar Check** dialog box.
- **`Shift + F7` (Writer: `Ctrl + F7`):** Opens the **Thesaurus (शब्दकोश)**, providing a list of synonyms (पर्यायवाची) and antonyms (विलोम शब्द) for the selected word.
- **`Shift + F7` (in Writer):** Automatic Spell Checking toggle.

---

## 2. Find and Replace (ढूंढना और बदलना)

When drafting legal affidavits or institute notices, you might need to change a company name across a 50-page document:

- **Find (खोजें):** **`Ctrl + F`**
  - Opens the Find bar at the bottom of the screen to highlight every occurrence of a specific word or phrase.
- **Find and Replace (खोजें और बदलें):** **`Ctrl + H`**
  - Prompts you for two inputs:
    - *Find:* The word you want to locate (e.g. `2025`).
    - *Replace With:* The new word you want to substitute (e.g. `2026`).
  - **Replace:** Replaces only the currently highlighted occurrence.
  - **Replace All:** Scans the entire document and substitutes every single occurrence in a split second.
  - **Match Case (केस मिलान):** When checked, respects capital and small letters strictly (e.g. searching for "Apple" will ignore "apple").

---

## 3. AutoCorrect and AutoText

- **AutoCorrect (स्वतः सुधार):** Automatically corrects common typing errors instantly as soon as you press the spacebar.
  - For example, typing `teh` automatically corrects to `the`.
  - Typing `(c)` automatically converts to the copyright symbol `©`.
- **AutoText in LibreOffice Writer:**
  - Shortcut: **`F3`**
  - Allows you to save long repetitive boilerplate paragraphs (such as company disclaimers or bank terms) and insert them instantly by typing a short shortcut word followed by `F3`.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which function key is used to start the Spelling and Grammar check in both LibreOffice Writer and MS Word?
A. `F1`
B. `F5`
C. `F7`
D. `F12`
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** `F7` is the universal industry standard function key to initiate the interactive Spelling and Grammar checking dialog.

---

### 2. What does a red wavy line underneath a word signify in a text document?
A. Grammatical error
B. Spelling error
C. Formatting error
D. Hyperlink
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A red wavy underline indicates that the word is not recognized in the software's active dictionary and is flagged as a potential spelling error.

---

### 3. Which keyboard shortcut opens the "Find and Replace" dialog box?
A. `Ctrl + F`
B. `Ctrl + H`
C. `Ctrl + R`
D. `Ctrl + G`
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** `Ctrl + H` opens the "Find and Replace" utility. (`Ctrl + F` only opens Find).

---

### 4. What is the keyboard shortcut to open the Thesaurus (synonyms dictionary) in LibreOffice Writer?
A. `Ctrl + F7`
B. `Shift + F7`
C. `F7`
D. `Alt + F7`
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** In LibreOffice Writer, `Ctrl + F7` opens the Thesaurus to find synonyms and antonyms. In MS Word, `Shift + F7` is used.

---

### 5. Which feature automatically replaces common misspellings (like changing "adn" to "and") the moment you hit the spacebar?
A. AutoFormat
B. AutoCorrect
C. AutoText
D. Mail Merge
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** AutoCorrect identifies pre-configured typographical errors as you type and automatically replaces them with their correct dictionary spellings immediately upon pressing the spacebar.
