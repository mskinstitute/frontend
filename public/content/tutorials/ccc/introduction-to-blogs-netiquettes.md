# Introduction to Blogs and Netiquettes (ब्लॉग और नेटीकेट्स)

The digital online space connects millions of people across diverse cultures. To maintain constructive, polite, and safe digital discourse, internet users must follow essential digital manners known as **Netiquettes**, and understand public publishing platforms called **Blogs**.

---

## 1. What is a Blog? (ब्लॉग क्या है?)

The word **Blog** is a shortened combination of **Weblog** (Web + Log). 

A **Blog** is a regularly updated personal or educational website run by an individual or a small team, written in an informal, conversational style where new articles (called **Blog Posts**) appear in **Reverse Chronological Order** (the newest post appears first at the top!).

- **Blogger (ब्लॉगर):** The person who writes and publishes blog posts.
- **Blogging (ब्लॉगिंग):** The activity of maintaining and writing a blog.
- **Vlog (Video Blog):** A blog where the primary medium is video (popular on YouTube).
- **Popular Free Blogging Platforms:** WordPress, Google Blogger (`blogspot.com`), Medium.

---

## 2. What are Netiquettes? (नेटीकेट्स - इंटरनेट शिष्टाचार)

**Netiquette** is a portmanteau of **Network + Etiquette** (नेटवर्क + शिष्टाचार). It refers to the informal code of good manners, respect, and professional conduct that individuals must observe when communicating over the internet.

### Golden Rules of Netiquette:
1. **Never Type in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS (कैपिटल लेटर्स में न लिखें):**
   - In digital communications, TYPING IN ALL CAPS IS CONSIDERED THE EQUIVALENT OF **SHOUTING OR YELLING (चीखना/चिल्लाना)** at someone! Always use normal sentence casing.
2. **Respect Other People's Privacy:** Never forward someone's private email, photos, or mobile numbers to public groups without their explicit permission.
3. **Do Not Spread Unverified Rumors (Fake News):** Never forward sensational WhatsApp forwards without verifying facts from official government sources (PIB Fact Check).
4. **Be Polite and Professional:** Avoid harsh abusive language, cyberbullying, or "flaming" (starting angry flame wars in comment sections).
5. **Respect People's Time and Bandwidth:** Do not spam groups with 50 morning stickers, good morning greeting GIFs, or massive video files.
6. **Acknowledge and Credit Sources:** If you use someone else's research, photos, or quote, always provide attribution to avoid copyright infringement.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What two words are combined to form the term "Blog"?
A. Book + Log
B. Web + Log
C. Base + Log
D. Broad + Log
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The term "Blog" is derived from the contraction of the phrase "Weblog" (Web + Log), coined in the late 1990s.

---

### 2. In internet communication and netiquette, what does typing messages in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS signify?
A. Urgent official priority
B. Shouting or yelling at the recipient
C. Computer programming code
D. High grammatical accuracy
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Writing in ALL CAPS online is universally interpreted as aggressive shouting or screaming and is considered poor netiquette.

---

### 3. In what chronological order do posts appear on a standard blog?
A. Alphabetical order
B. Reverse chronological order (newest posts appear at the top)
C. Random order
D. Length of the article
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Blogs present entries in reverse chronological order, meaning the most recent article is featured at the top of the feed.

---

### 4. What does the term "Netiquette" represent?
A. Network security firewall
B. Online etiquette and polite rules of behavior for digital communications
C. Internet speed measurement tool
D. Computer hardware cabling standard
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Netiquette (Network + Etiquette) encompasses the social conventions, courtesies, and professional ethics expected when communicating online.

---

### 5. What is a blog whose content is delivered predominantly through video called?
A. Podcast
B. Vlog (Video Blog)
C. Wiki
D. Forum
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Vlog (Video Blog) is a format of blogging where thoughts, travelogues, and tutorials are presented via recorded video rather than written text.
