# Introduction to OTP and QR Code (OTP और क्यूआर कोड)

With the explosive growth of online shopping, digital banking, and instant mobile payments under Digital India, understanding security tokens like **OTP** and matrix barcodes like **QR Codes** is vital for digital literacy.

## 1. What is an OTP? (One Time Password - ओटीपी)

An **OTP (One Time Password)** is an automatically generated numeric or alphanumeric string of characters (typically **4 to 6 digits**) that authenticates a single transaction or login session.

### Key Characteristics of OTP (CCC Super Important):
1. **Dynamic (गतिशील):** Unlike your permanent ATM PIN or email password (which stays the same until you change it), a fresh, unique OTP is generated for **each specific transaction**.
2. **Short Validity (सीमित समय):** An OTP remains valid only for a short time window—typically **3 to 10 minutes**—after which it automatically expires.
3. **Single Use Only:** Once entered to confirm a transaction, that specific OTP becomes permanently invalid; it cannot be reused.
4. **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** OTP acts as the second layer of security. Even if a thief steals your net banking password, they cannot steal your money without the secret OTP sent to your registered mobile number!

> **CRITICAL CYBER SAFETY RULE:**
> **Never share your OTP with anyone!** Bank managers, police officers, or payment customer care agents will **NEVER** ask for your OTP. Sharing an OTP gives the caller direct access to withdraw money from your bank account!

## 2. What is a QR Code? (Quick Response Code - क्यूआर कोड)

A **QR Code (Quick Response Code)** is a **two-dimensional (2D) matrix barcode** composed of black squares arranged in a square grid on a white background.

### History & Invention:
- Invented in **1994** by the Japanese automotive company **Denso Wave** (lead engineer **Masahiro Hara**) to track automobile parts in Toyota factories.
- Traditional 1D barcodes (linear vertical lines) can only store about 20 numeric characters horizontally.
- **2D QR Codes can store over 7,000 numeric digits or 4,000 alphanumeric characters** both horizontally and vertically!
- **Position Detection Patterns:** Notice the **three large square finder patterns** in the corners (top-left, top-right, bottom-left). They allow smartphones to scan and decode the QR code from any angle (even upside down)!

### What is BharatQR?
**BharatQR** is a common, interoperable digital payment QR code standard developed jointly by **NPCI**, Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Merchants only need to display a single BharatQR stand on their counter to accept payments from BHIM UPI, Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, and bank debit cards!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What does the acronym "OTP" stand for in digital transactions?
A. Only Transaction Password
B. One Time Password
C. Online Telecommunication Protocol
D. Official Transfer Permit
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** OTP stands for One Time Password. It is an automatically generated security string valid for a single transaction or login attempt.

---

### 2. Who invented the QR Code in 1994?
A. Tim Berners-Lee
B. Denso Wave (Masahiro Hara)
C. Bill Gates
D. Alan Turing
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The QR Code was invented in 1994 by Masahiro Hara at the Japanese company Denso Wave to track automotive components during vehicle assembly.

---

### 3. What is the typical validity duration of a banking transaction OTP?
A. 24 Hours
B. 3 to 10 Minutes
C. 1 Month
D. Permanent
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Banking and financial transaction OTPs are strictly time-bound for security, typically expiring within 3 to 10 minutes from issuance.

---

### 4. What is the primary advantage of a 2D QR Code over a traditional 1D linear barcode?
A. QR codes require no electricity
B. QR codes store exponentially more data (both horizontally and vertically) and can be scanned from any angle
C. QR codes only work in Japan
D. QR codes cannot be photocopied
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Unlike 1D barcodes that encode data linearly in one direction, 2D QR codes encode information both horizontally and vertically, holding thousands of characters with omnidirectional scanning.

---

### 5. What should you do if an unknown caller claiming to be your SBI Bank Manager asks for your OTP to "unblock your ATM card"?
A. Share the OTP immediately
B. Never share the OTP and report the fraud attempt to the bank and cyber crime helpline (1930)
C. Transfer 500 rupees
D. Send the OTP via SMS
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Banks explicitly state that official personnel will never request an OTP. Legitimate transactions never require sharing OTPs with third-party callers.
