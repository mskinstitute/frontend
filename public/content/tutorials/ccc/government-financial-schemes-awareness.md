# Government Financial Schemes and Awareness (सरकारी वित्तीय योजनाएं)

To bring hundreds of millions of unbanked citizens into the formal financial mainstream, the Government of India launched the **National Mission for Financial Inclusion** (वित्तीय समावेशन).

In the NIELIT CCC exam, questions testing **PMJDY**, **PMSBY**, **PMJJBY**, and **APY** appear consistently!

---

## 1. Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY - जन धन योजना)

- Launched on **28 August 2014** by Prime Minister Narendra Modi with the slogan: *"Mera Khata, Bhagya Vidhata"* (मेरा खाता, भाग्य विधाता).
- **Core Features of PMJDY:**
  1. **Zero Balance Account:** No minimum balance required to maintain the account (शून्य बैलेंस खाता).
  2. **Free RuPay Debit Card:** Comes with built-in **Accidental Insurance Cover of ₹2,00,000** (for accounts opened after 28.08.2018; ₹1 Lakh for older accounts).
  3. **Overdraft Facility (OD):** Eligible account holders can avail an overdraft credit limit up to **₹10,000** (subject to conditions).
  4. **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT):** Government subsidies (LPG subsidy, PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi, student scholarships) are deposited directly into the beneficiary's Jan Dhan bank account.

---

## 2. Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY - सुरक्षा बीमा योजना)

- An ultra-affordable **Accidental Death & Disability Insurance Scheme**.
- **Eligible Age Group:** **18 to 70 Years**.
- **Annual Premium (सालाना प्रीमियम):** Only **`₹20 per year`** (revised from ₹12/year). Deducted automatically via auto-debit from your savings account once a year.
- **Insurance Coverage:**
  - Accidental Death or Permanent Total Disability: **`₹2,00,000` (2 Lakhs)**.
  - Permanent Partial Disability (loss of one eye/limb): **`₹1,00,000` (1 Lakh)**.

---

## 3. Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY - जीवन ज्योति बीमा)

- A government-backed **Term Life Insurance Scheme** covering death due to **ANY cause** (accidental or natural/illness).
- **Eligible Age Group:** **18 to 50 Years** (risk cover continues up to age 55).
- **Annual Premium:** **`₹436 per year`** (revised from ₹330/year). Auto-debited annually.
- **Life Insurance Sum Assured:** **`₹2,00,000` (2 Lakhs)** paid to the nominee upon the death of the insured person.

---

## 4. Atal Pension Yojana (APY - अटल पेंशन योजना)

- A guaranteed pension scheme launched in 2015 focused on workers in the **unorganized sector** (daily wage laborers, farmers, domestic helpers, shop assistants).
- Administered by **PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority)**.
- **Eligible Age of Entry:** **18 to 40 Years**.
- **Pension Benefit:** Subscribers receive a **guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000, or ₹5,000 per month** after reaching the age of **60 years**, depending on their monthly contribution during youth.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is the eligible age entry criteria for the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)?
A. 10 to 50 years
B. 18 to 70 years
C. 21 to 60 years
D. 25 to 65 years
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Citizens between the ages of 18 and 70 years possessing an active bank savings account are eligible to enroll in the PMSBY accidental insurance scheme.

---

### 2. What is the current annual premium for the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)?
A. ₹12 per year
B. ₹20 per year
C. ₹330 per year
D. ₹436 per year
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The annual premium for PMSBY accidental insurance was revised to ₹20 per annum (auto-debited from the subscriber's account).

---

### 3. What is the maximum guaranteed monthly pension amount available under the Atal Pension Yojana (APY) after attaining 60 years of age?
A. ₹2,000 per month
B. ₹5,000 per month
C. ₹10,000 per month
D. ₹25,000 per month
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Under the Atal Pension Yojana, subscribers choose a fixed guaranteed monthly pension tier between ₹1,000 and ₹5,000 per month payable upon turning 60.

---

### 4. What is the current annual premium for the Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)?
A. ₹20 per year
B. ₹436 per year
C. ₹100 per year
D. ₹1,000 per year
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The annual premium for PMJJBY term life insurance is ₹436 per year, providing life cover of ₹2,00,000 for any cause of death.

---

### 5. What slogan was associated with the launch of the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)?
A. Digital India, Swachh India
B. Mera Khata, Bhagya Vidhata
C. Padhe Bharat, Badhe Bharat
D. Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The official motivational slogan of PMJDY financial inclusion mission is "Mera Khata, Bhagya Vidhata" (My Account, Creator of My Fortune).
