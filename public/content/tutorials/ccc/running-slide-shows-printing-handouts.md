# Running Slide Shows and Printing Handouts (स्लाइड शो चलाना और हैंडआउट्स)

Once slides, transitions, and bullet points are prepared, the final step is delivering the live presentation to the audience using **Slide Show mode** and printing physical **Handouts** for study notes.

---

## 1. Running a Slide Show: Function Key Shortcuts

Memorize these two function keys—they appear in almost every NIELIT CCC exam paper:

| Command | Action Performed | Keyboard Shortcut |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Start Slide Show from the Beginning** | Launches presentation from **Slide 1** in full-screen mode | **`F5`** |
| **Start Slide Show from Current Slide** | Launches presentation from the **currently selected slide** | **`Shift + F5`** |
| **Exit Slide Show** | Immediately terminates full-screen mode and returns to editor | **`Esc`** |
| **Advance to Next Slide** | Move forward | **`Spacebar` / `Enter` / `Page Down` / $ightarrow$** |
| **Return to Previous Slide** | Move backward | **`Backspace` / `Page Up` / $leftarrow$** |
| **Black Screen Toggle** | Turns the screen completely black to focus audience attention | **`B`** |
| **White Screen Toggle** | Turns the screen completely white | **`W`** |

---

## 2. What is Presenter View? (प्रस्तुतकर्ता दृश्य)

When you connect your laptop to an external digital projector:
- **The Audience Screen (Projector):** Displays only the clean, full-screen slide.
- **Your Laptop Screen (Presenter View):** Displays the current slide, **your private speaking notes**, a preview of the upcoming next slide, and an active elapsed presentation stopwatch timer!

---

## 3. Printing Handouts for Students (हैंडआउट्स प्रिंट करना)

In seminars and computer institutes like MSK Institute, teachers distribute printed paper copies of presentation slides to students so they can take notes. These are called **Handouts (हैंडआउट्स)**.

### Handout Layout Options:
In the Print dialog box (`Ctrl + P`), under **Print Content**, select **Handouts**:
- **1 Slide per page:** Large full-page slide prints.
- **3 Slides per page (Most Popular!):** Prints 3 slide miniatures on the left side, with **blank ruled lines on the right side** for students to write handwritten notes!
- **6 or 9 Slides per page:** Compact prints to save paper.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which function key starts a Slide Show from the very first slide (Slide 1)?
A. `F1`
B. `F5`
C. `Shift + F5`
D. `F7`
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Pressing `F5` initiates the full-screen Slide Show starting from the very first slide of the presentation.

---

### 2. Which keyboard shortcut launches a Slide Show starting directly from the CURRENT active slide?
A. `F5`
B. `Shift + F5`
C. `Ctrl + F5`
D. `Alt + F5`
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Pressing `Shift + F5` launches the presentation directly from the currently highlighted slide, bypassing preceding slides.

---

### 3. Which key on the keyboard immediately terminates and exits a full-screen Slide Show?
A. `Enter`
B. `Esc`
C. `Spacebar`
D. `Delete`
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Pressing the `Esc` (Escape) key immediately halts full-screen presentation playback and returns to the normal editing workspace.

---

### 4. What are printed paper documents containing multiple miniature slide thumbnails distributed to audiences called?
A. Blueprints
B. Handouts
C. Proofs
D. Transcripts
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Handouts are paper printouts of presentation slides (often arranged 3, 6, or 9 slides per page) given to participants for reference and note-taking.

---

### 5. What happens when you press the `B` key on your keyboard during an active full-screen Slide Show?
A. The presentation is deleted
B. The screen turns completely black to draw audience attention to the speaker
C. It applies bold formatting
D. The show restarts from slide 1
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Pressing `B` during a slide show toggles a black screen (and `W` toggles a white screen), temporarily hiding visual slides so audiences focus on the speaker.
