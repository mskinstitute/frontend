# Advanced Indentation & Spacing

## Precision Alignment: The Tabs Dialog & Dot Leaders

Novice users often press the spacebar repeatedly to align prices, dates, or signatures on the right side of a line. This is the cardinal sin of word processing—as soon as fonts or margins change, the alignment disintegrates!

Professional documents use **Tab Stops** and **Leader Dots**.

![Advanced Tabs and Dot Leaders in Word](/images/tutorials/ms-word/home-paragraph-formatting.svg)

### 1. Types of Tab Stops

When you press the **Tab** key, the cursor leaps to the next defined Tab Stop:

1. **Left Tab (⫷)**: Text extends to the right from the tab stop.
2. **Center Tab (⫾)**: Text centers evenly on the tab stop mark.
3. **Right Tab (⫸)**: Text extends to the left from the tab stop (perfect for flush right dates and prices).
4. **Decimal Tab (⫸.)**: Aligns numbers precisely along their **decimal point**! Whether a number is `₹5.50` or `₹15,450.75`, all decimal dots line up in a flawless vertical column.
5. **Bar Tab (|)**: Draws an automatic vertical divider line at the tab position without indenting text.

### 2. The Tabs Dialog Box

To configure precise tab stops:
1. Open the Paragraph dialog box (`Home > Paragraph ↘`).
2. Click the **Tabs...** button at the bottom-left.
3. In the Tabs dialog box:
   - **Tab stop position**: Type exact ruler position (e.g. `6.5 in` for the right margin of a standard letter).
   - **Alignment**: Choose **Right** (or Decimal).
   - **Leader**: Choose leader style:
     - `1 None`
     - `2 .....` (Classic dotted leader for menus, agendas, and tables of contents)
     - `3 -----` (Dashed rule)
     - `4 _____` (Solid underline, perfect for fill-in blank signature lines!)
4. Click **Set**, then click **OK**.

### 3. Using Tab Leaders in Real Life

Now type your item name:
- Type: `Executive Laptop Stand`
- Press the **Tab** key once!
- Word automatically draws a flawless dotted trail `...........................` across the page all the way to 6.5" and aligns your price `₹1,250` flush against the right margin!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which type of Tab Stop aligns numbers vertically along their decimal points regardless of how many digits they contain?
A. Left Tab
B. Right Tab
C. Decimal Tab
D. Bar Tab
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A Decimal Tab aligns numbers along the decimal period (.) for clean accounting ledgers.
---

### 2. What is a "Tab Leader" in Microsoft Word?
A. The first tab on the Ribbon
B. A repeated row of dots (.....) or dashes bridging text to a tab stop
C. The team leader's name
D. A table column header
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Tab Leaders draw automated rows of dots, dashes, or solid lines connecting items to right-aligned values.
---

### 3. Which tab leader option (Option 4 in the Tabs dialog) creates an instant solid line for fill-in signatures?
A. None
B. Dotted leader (2)
C. Dashed leader (3)
D. Solid underline leader (4)
**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Leader style 4 draws a solid horizontal underline rule across to the tab stop for blank fill-in lines.
---

### 4. Where can you open the dedicated Tabs configuration dialog box in MS Word?
A. View > Zoom
B. Paragraph dialog box > Tabs... button at bottom-left
C. Insert > Table
D. Design > Themes
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Clicking the "Tabs..." button inside the Paragraph dialog opens the precision tab configuration window.
---

### 5. What visual tab stop type inserts an automated vertical dividing rule line down the page?
A. Left Tab
B. Bar Tab
C. Center Tab
D. Decimal Tab
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Bar Tab places a vertical divider bar at the tab position without shifting text.
