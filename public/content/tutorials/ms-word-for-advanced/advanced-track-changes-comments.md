# Advanced Track Changes & Comments

## Multi-Author Collaboration: Advanced Tracking

When contracts, technical manuals, or corporate policies undergo review by multiple stakeholders (e.g. Legal Counsel, Finance Manager, and Lead Architect), managing who changed what and preventing unauthorized tracking overrides requires advanced features.

![Advanced Track Changes and Reviewer Filtering in Word](/images/tutorials/ms-word/track-changes-review.svg)

### 1. Filtering Revisions by Specific Reviewer

When 4 different people have edited a 100-page document:
1. Go to **Review** tab > **Tracking** group.
2. Click **Show Markup** > hover over **Specific People**.
3. Word displays a checklist of all contributing editors.
4. **Uncheck "All Reviewers"** and check **only "Legal Counsel"**:
   - The canvas instantly hides all other editors' markup, allowing you to focus solely on legal revisions!
   - You can review, accept, or reject legal amendments in isolation without touching engineering or finance comments.

### 2. Locking Track Changes with a Password

A dangerous risk during formal negotiations is a counterparty secretly turning off Track Changes and inserting unapproved terms into the contract.
**Lock Tracking** eliminates this vulnerability:
1. On the **Review** tab, click the downward arrow beneath the **Track Changes** button.
2. Click **Lock Tracking**.
3. Enter and confirm a secure password.
4. Click **OK**.
5. *Result*: The Track Changes button is now **locked in the active ON state** and grayed out! Neither you nor any recipient can disable revision tracking or accept changes without entering the master password!

### 3. Advanced Track Changes Options

Click the Dialog Box Launcher (`↘`) in the **Review > Tracking** group:
- **Advanced Options**: Customize exact color codings:
  - Set *Insertions* to Bold Green.
  - Set *Deletions* to Strikethrough Red.
  - Set *Moved Text* to Green with double strikethrough.
- **Balloons**: Configure whether revisions appear inline or float in the right margin margin pane.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which security feature prevents reviewers or external parties from disabling Track Changes in a shared document?
A. Read-only file mode
B. Lock Tracking with a password
C. Protect Document with Watermark
D. Save as PDF
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Lock Tracking encrypts the tracking engine so nobody can turn off Track Changes without the password.
---

### 2. How can you isolate and view revisions made solely by one specific person in a multi-author document?
A. Delete all other comments
B. Show Markup > Specific People > Check only that reviewer
C. Copy text to Notepad
D. Search reviewer name in Find
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Show Markup > Specific People filters displayed revisions and comments by individual author names.
---

### 3. What happens to the Track Changes button on the Ribbon once "Lock Tracking" is enabled with a password?
A. It disappears completely
B. It remains locked in the active ON state and grayed out
C. It turns into a stop sign
D. It prompts for a password every 5 minutes
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The button becomes locked in the ON state, preventing any user from disabling revision tracking.
---

### 4. Under which Ribbon tab is the Lock Tracking command located?
A. Home
B. Developer
C. Review
D. View
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Review > Tracking dropdown houses the Lock Tracking security feature.
---

### 5. Can you customize the colors assigned to insertions and deletions in Word Track Changes?
A. No, colors are fixed by Microsoft
B. Yes, through Advanced Track Changes Options
C. Only in black and white
D. Requires third-party software
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The Advanced Options dialog in Tracking allows choosing exact colors for insertions, deletions, and moved text.
