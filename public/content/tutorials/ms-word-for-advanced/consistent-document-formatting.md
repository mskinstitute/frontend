# Consistent Document Formatting

## Pagination & Typographic Flow Rules

In professional book design and report publishing, nothing looks more amateurish than an isolated single line of text stranded at the top or bottom of a page. Microsoft Word provides sophisticated typographic pagination rules to enforce layout harmony.

### 1. Widows and Orphans Explained

- **Widow**: The **last line** of a paragraph printed all by itself at the **top** of the next page.
- **Orphan**: The **first line** of a paragraph stranded all by itself at the **bottom** of a page before the rest wraps over.
- **Widow/Orphan Control (Always Keep On!)**:
  - Open the Paragraph dialog box (`Ctrl + D` > Paragraph, or click `↘` in Home > Paragraph).
  - Switch to the **Line and Page Breaks** tab.
  - Check **Widow/Orphan control**.
  - Word automatically ensures at least **two lines** of a paragraph always appear together at the top or bottom of any page!

### 2. Essential Pagination Flags

| Rule | Where to Apply | Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Keep with next** | Headings (`Heading 1, 2, 3`) | Glues the heading to the subsequent paragraph; never leaves a heading alone at the bottom of a page! |
| **Keep lines together** | Legal signature blocks, short code snippets | Prevents a paragraph from splitting across pages under any circumstances. |
| **Page break before** | Chapter titles | Guarantees that major section headings always start fresh at the top of a new page automatically! |

### 3. Style Sets & Quick Document Design

On the **Design** tab, the **Document Formatting** gallery provides pre-configured **Style Sets**:
- *Modern*, *Casual*, *Centred*, *Formal*, *Technical*, and *Minimalist*.
- Selecting a Style Set restructures spacing, heading underlines, and decorative accents across your entire document while preserving all your text hierarchy!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is an "orphan" in professional typography and word processing?
A. A missing image
B. The first line of a paragraph stranded alone at the bottom of a page
C. A deleted file
D. A blank table cell
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** An orphan is the single first line of a paragraph sitting alone at the bottom margin before wrapping.
---

### 2. Which checkbox on the Line and Page Breaks tab forces a heading to always start at the very top of a new page?
A. Keep lines together
B. Page break before
C. Widow control
D. Suppress line numbers
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** "Page break before" automatically triggers a fresh page break immediately before the styled element.
---

### 3. Which rule prevents a 3-line legal signature block from splitting across two different pages?
A. Keep lines together
B. Double spacing
C. Justify text
D. Center align
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** "Keep lines together" prevents page breaks from dissecting the interior lines of the selected paragraph.
---

### 4. How many lines of text does "Widow/Orphan control" ensure must stay together at page boundaries?
A. At least 1 line
B. At least 2 lines
C. At least 5 lines
D. The entire paragraph
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Widow/Orphan control guarantees a minimum of two lines at the top or bottom of a page.
---

### 5. Where are the pagination control checkboxes located in Microsoft Word?
A. Insert > Page Number
B. Paragraph dialog box > Line and Page Breaks tab
C. Review > Proofing
D. View > Split
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The Paragraph dialog box contains the "Line and Page Breaks" tab with all flow rules.
