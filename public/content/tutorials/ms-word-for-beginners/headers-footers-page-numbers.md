# Headers, Footers & Page Numbers

## Headers, Footers & Automated Page Numbers

Headers and footers run consistently across the top and bottom margins of your pages. They contain essential metadata: document titles, company names, revision dates, chapter names, and automated page numbers.

### Activating the Header & Footer

- **Fastest Method**: **Double-click** inside the top or bottom 1-inch blank margin of any page.
- **Ribbon Method**: Go to **Insert** tab > click **Header**, **Footer**, or **Page Number**.
- **Exit Header & Footer**: Double-click back into the document body, or click the red **Close Header and Footer** button on the Ribbon (or press `Esc`).

### Inserting Automated Page Numbers

Never type page numbers manually! If you manually type "1", every page will display "1"!
Instead, use Word's dynamic fields:
1. Go to **Insert > Page Number** (or click inside footer > Header & Footer tab > Page Number).
2. Choose position:
   - **Bottom of Page**: Center, Right, or Left.
   - **Page X of Y (Bold Numbers)**: Displays "Page 3 of 42", giving readers context of document length.
3. **Format Page Numbers**:
   - Change number formats to Roman numerals (`i, ii, iii`) or Alphabetic letters (`a, b, c`).
   - Choose **Start at**: e.g., start numbering from 0 or 1.

### Essential Header & Footer Options

When inside the Header/Footer area, the **Header & Footer** contextual tab opens:

1. **Different First Page**:
   - Checks this box to completely clear the header and footer on Page 1!
   - Vital for cover pages, title sheets, and formal reports where you do not want page numbers or logos displayed on the front cover.
2. **Different Odd & Even Pages**:
   - Enables mirror headers for bound books (e.g., Book Title on left-hand even pages, Chapter Title on right-hand odd pages).
3. **Link to Previous**:
   - When documents are divided into sections, this button controls whether Section 2 inherits Section 1's header.
   - **Unlinking** allows you to have Roman numerals (`i, ii, iii`) in Section 1 (Table of Contents) and Arabic numbers (`1, 2, 3`) starting fresh in Section 2!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. How can you quickly open the Header or Footer area of any page for editing without using ribbon menus?
A. Right-click anywhere
B. Double-click in the top or bottom margin
C. Press Ctrl + H
D. Press F5
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Double-clicking inside the top or bottom margin instantly activates the Header & Footer editor.
---

### 2. Which checkbox hides headers and footers specifically on the title cover page of a document?
A. Hide Header
B. Different First Page
C. Different Odd & Even
D. Remove Page 1
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** "Different First Page" isolates Page 1, allowing cover pages to remain clean without headers or numbers.
---

### 3. Why should you never manually type numbers like "1" into a footer?
A. Word will report an error
B. The number "1" will appear on every single page of the document
C. Printers refuse to print manual numbers
D. It deletes the footer
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Typing static numbers duplicates that exact text on every page; dynamic Page Number fields must be used instead.
---

### 4. Which button must be toggled off to allow Section 2 to have a different header than Section 1?
A. Repeat Header
B. Link to Previous
C. Header Sync
D. Inherit Format
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Clicking "Link to Previous" breaks the inheritance between sections, allowing distinct headers.
---

### 5. What key exits the Header and Footer editing mode and returns cursor focus to the body canvas?
A. Enter
B. Esc
C. Ctrl + W
D. Tab
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Pressing the Esc key immediately closes Header and Footer mode and returns to body text.
