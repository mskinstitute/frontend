# Designing a Report

## Mini-Project 2: Designing a Formal Business Report

A business report communicates strategic findings, financial audits, or technical proposals to executives and clients. In this hands-on project, you will build a comprehensive, multi-page business report.

### Step 1: Adding a Built-in Cover Page
1. Open a blank document.
2. Go to **Insert** tab > **Pages** group > click **Cover Page**.
3. Select a modern template (e.g. *Facet*, *Retrospect*, or *Integral*).
4. Fill in:
   - Report Title: `QUARTERLY BUSINESS EXPANSION PROPOSAL`
   - Subtitle: `Strategic Roadmap & Financial Forecast`
   - Author: `Sumit Kumar, Lead Architect`
   - Organization: `MSK Institute of Technology`
   - Date: Pick from date dropdown.

### Step 2: Isolating Sections with Breaks
1. At the end of the Cover Page, insert a **Section Break (Next Page)** via **Layout > Breaks > Next Page**.
2. This ensures the cover page remains completely independent from subsequent pages.

### Step 3: Generating the Interactive Table of Contents
1. On Page 2, type the header `Table of Contents` in Heading 1.
2. Go to **References > Table of Contents > Automatic Table 1**.
3. Insert another **Page Break (`Ctrl + Enter`)** to begin the Executive Summary on Page 3.

### Step 4: Structuring Report Body with Styles
Apply consistent heading hierarchy throughout the document:
- `Executive Summary` (Heading 1)
- `Market Opportunity Analysis` (Heading 1)
  - `Target Demographics` (Heading 2)
  - `Competitor Benchmarks` (Heading 2)
- `Financial Projections` (Heading 1)
  - Insert a formatted data table with zebra striping (**Banded Rows**) and `Repeat Header Rows` enabled!

### Step 5: Professional Headers & Footers
1. Double-click the footer area on Page 2.
2. Uncheck **Link to Previous** so the cover page stays blank.
3. Check **Different First Page** if needed.
4. Insert **Page Number > Bottom of Page > Page X of Y**.
5. Close Header & Footer.

### Step 6: Final Polish & Spell Check (`F7`)
1. Press **F7** to run the complete Spelling & Grammar proofing engine.
2. Press **F9** on the Table of Contents to update all page numbers.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Under which Ribbon tab is the pre-built "Cover Page" gallery located?
A. Layout
B. Insert
C. Design
D. References
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Insert > Pages > Cover Page provides pre-designed title pages for business documents.
---

### 2. Which break type should be used immediately after a cover page to isolate headers and footers?
A. Page Break
B. Section Break (Next Page)
C. Column Break
D. Soft Line Break
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A Section Break (Next Page) creates an isolated section so headers and footers do not bleed onto the cover.
---

### 3. What function key launches the complete Spelling and Grammar checking engine in MS Word?
A. F1
B. F5
C. F7
D. F12
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** F7 is the universal shortcut to start the Spelling and Grammar proofing tool.
---

### 4. What page numbering style is recommended for formal reports to give readers full length context?
A. Roman only
B. Page X of Y
C. Letters only
D. No numbers
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** "Page X of Y" displays both the current page and total page count for complete clarity.
---

### 5. How do you refresh the Table of Contents after making edits to report body text?
A. Delete and retype the table
B. Select TOC and press F9 (or click Update Table)
C. Restart Word
D. Change font size
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Clicking Update Table or pressing F9 refreshes the Table of Contents with updated page numbers.
---
