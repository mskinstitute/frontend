# Capstone Project: Build an Interactive Sales Dashboard

*Reading Time: 8 Mins* | *Level: Beginner to Advanced*

---

## 1. Project Blueprint: The Executive Sales Dashboard
In this capstone project, we assemble a complete, production-ready Sales Analytics Dashboard incorporating:
1. **Header Ribbon:** Company branding, Title, and Date slicer.
2. **Top KPI Row:** 4 Card visuals (Total Revenue, Total Units Sold, Average Order Value, Profit Margin %).
3. **Core Analytics (Middle):**
   - Monthly Revenue Trajectory (Line Chart with target line).
   - Category Breakdown (Horizontal Bar Chart).
4. **Geographic & Customer Insights (Bottom):**
   - Regional Map / Matrix showing City-level sales.
   - Dynamic Drillthrough to Customer profile details.

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is the first step when starting a new end-to-end Power BI dashboard project?
A. Ingesting raw data into Power Query to audit quality, clean missing data, and establish correct datatypes
B. Picking chart colors
C. Writing 50 DAX measures
D. Publishing to the cloud
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A successful BI project begins with data validation and preparation in Power Query.
---

### 2. What design layout ensures that KPI cards look cohesive and structured across the top of a canvas?
A. Uniform spacing, aligned tops, matching card heights, and consistent callout font sizes
B. Random staggering
C. Overlapping cards
D. Making each card a different font
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Geometric alignment and consistent typography create an orderly, professional visual rhythm.
---

### 3. What feature allows an executive to right-click a bar (e.g. 'Mumbai') and navigate to a dedicated sub-page showing detailed customer orders for that city?
A. Drillthrough
B. Hyperlink
C. Zoom
D. Bookmark
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Drillthrough filters a dedicated target detail page based on the selected data point.
---

### 4. Why should background grids and axes be formatted in subtle muted tones?
A. To keep visual focus squarely on the data curves and KPI numbers rather than decorative scaffolding
B. To save ink
C. Because Power BI requires grey
D. It increases PC speed
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Subdued structural elements reduce visual clutter and emphasize data insights.
---

### 5. What is the ultimate metric of a successful business intelligence dashboard?
A. Whether business leaders can rapidly make confident, data-backed decisions that drive growth and operational efficiency
B. How many charts are on the screen
C. How large the file is
D. How complex the DAX code looks
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Business impact, decision velocity, and user adoption are the true measures of BI success.
