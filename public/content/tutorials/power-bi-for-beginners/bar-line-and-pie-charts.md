# Bar, Line, and Pie Charts in Power BI

*Reading Time: 8 Mins* | *Level: Beginner to Advanced*

---

## 1. Core Visuals in Power BI
- **Clustered & Stacked Bar Charts:** Ideal for categorical comparisons and part-to-whole segmentations.
- **Line Charts & Area Charts:** Best for displaying temporal trends over continuous date axes.
- **Donut & Pie Charts:** Useful strictly for 2 to 4 segments where differences are stark (e.g. Mobile vs Desktop). Avoid pie charts with more than 5 slices.

---

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which visual is best suited for showing sales trends across continuous months?
A. Line Chart
B. Pie Chart
C. Scatter Plot
D. Treemap
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Line charts connect sequential date periods, making trends, seasonal spikes, and dips immediately apparent.
---

### 2. Why should pie charts with more than 5 or 6 categories be avoided in executive reporting?
A. Slices become tiny slivers that are difficult for human eyes to compare accurately
B. Power BI crashes
C. Pie charts cannot have colors
D. Labels are deleted
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Comparing angles and areas of narrow slices creates high cognitive load; bar charts are more effective.
---

### 3. What feature in a Stacked Column Chart allows users to see the relative percentage contribution of each category totaling 100%?
A. 100% Stacked Column Chart
B. Normalization slider
C. Auto-percentage
D. Percent of total card
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The 100% Stacked Column Chart normalizes each bar height to 100%, emphasizing component proportions.
---

### 4. How can you enable data labels (numbers displayed directly above bars) on a column chart in Power BI?
A. In the Format pane under 'Data labels', toggle the switch to 'On'
B. Right-click the Windows desktop
C. Type DAX code
D. Re-import the data
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The Format Visual visual pane provides toggles for Data Labels, Titles, and Gridlines.
---

### 5. What does the 'Small Multiples' field well do on bar and line charts in Power BI?
A. Splits a single visual into a grid of smaller synchronized charts partitioned by a category (e.g. one small chart per region)
B. Reduces chart size
C. Multiplies values by 2
D. Adds extra decimals
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Small Multiples (trellis plot) partitions a chart across categorical values while maintaining shared scale axes.
