# Combining Multiple Visuals & Cross-Filtering

*Reading Time: 8 Mins* | *Level: Beginner to Advanced*

---

## 1. Cross-Filtering and Cross-Highlighting
In Power BI, visuals on a page are dynamically connected by default:
- **Cross-Filtering:** Clicking on a category (e.g., "Electronics") filters all other charts to display only Electronics data.
- **Cross-Highlighting:** Clicking on a category retains the full bar in muted grey and highlights the selected portion in vibrant color.

---

## 2. Edit Interactions: Controlling Visual Behavior
Sometimes you **do not** want a specific visual to filter another visual (e.g. an overall benchmark card should always show the full company total regardless of slicer clicks):
1. Select the visual.
2. Go to the **Format ribbon** $	o$ Click **Edit Interactions**.
3. Click the **None** icon ($oslash$) on visuals you want to decouple!

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What happens by default when a user clicks on the 'North' bar in a regional sales chart?
A. All other visuals on the canvas automatically cross-filter or cross-highlight to show data for the 'North' region
B. The chart is deleted
C. The page reloads
D. An error occurs
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Power BI's default interaction is automatic cross-filtering between visuals on the active page.
---

### 2. How do you prevent a slicer or chart from affecting a specific summary card visual?
A. Use 'Edit Interactions' on the Format ribbon and click the 'None' icon over the target visual
B. Lock the computer
C. Put the card on a different monitor
D. Unlink the database
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The 'Edit Interactions' tool allows report designers to configure whether visuals filter, highlight, or ignore selections.
---

### 3. What are the three interaction options available between visuals in Power BI?
A. Filter, Highlight, and None
B. Sum, Average, and Count
C. Add, Subtract, and Multiply
D. Open, Close, and Save
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Interacting visuals can be configured to Filter (show only filtered data), Highlight (show proportion of total), or None (ignore).
---

### 4. What tool groups multiple visual elements together so they can be moved, resized, or hidden as a single container?
A. Grouping (Ctrl + G or right-click > Group)
B. Merging
C. Locking
D. Bundling
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Grouping organizes multiple cards, shapes, or text boxes into unified visual containers.
---

### 5. In the Selection Pane, what does the eye icon next to a visual name do?
A. Toggles the visibility of the visual on the canvas (Hide / Show)
B. Zooms in
C. Changes the color
D. Deletes the visual
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The Selection Pane's eye icon toggles visual visibility, which is essential for creating interactive bookmark views.
