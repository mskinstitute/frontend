# Cards and KPIs in Power BI

*Reading Time: 8 Mins* | *Level: Beginner to Advanced*

---

## 1. The Power of Big Numbers: Card Visuals
The **Card Visual** displays a single critical business number (e.g., Total Revenue ₹45.2 Cr, Active Users 1.2M, NPS Score +64). It is the most important element on an executive dashboard.

---

## 2. The New Card Visual (Multi-Card Container)
In modern Power BI, the **New Card Visual** allows:
- Grouping multiple KPI cards into a clean horizontal container.
- Adding reference labels (e.g. "+14% vs Last Month").
- Adding dynamic accent bars and mini sparklines.

---

## 3. The KPI Visual
A specialized visual showing:
1. The **Value** (current metric).
2. The **Target / Goal** (benchmark).
3. The **Trend axis** (shaded background indicating trajectory).
4. Automatic color coding (Green if on target, Red if behind target).

# Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Which visual is designed to showcase a single high-level headline KPI (like Total Revenue) prominently at the top of a dashboard?
A. Card Visual
B. Scatter Plot
C. Treemap
D. Decomposition Tree
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Card visuals present single key figures clearly for executive visibility.
---

### 2. What three components make up a native Power BI KPI visual?
A. Current Value, Target Goal, and a Background Trend Axis
B. Username, Password, and Date
C. Pie chart, Bar chart, and Line chart
D. Title, Subtitle, and Logo
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The KPI visual requires an indicator value, a target goal, and a trend axis.
---

### 3. How can you display numbers in abbreviated format (e.g. ₹1.2M instead of ₹1,200,000) on a Card visual?
A. Set 'Display units' to 'Millions' in the Format pane under Callout value
B. Use a calculator
C. Divide by 100 in SQL
D. Power BI cannot abbreviate numbers
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The 'Display units' setting (Auto, Thousands, Millions, Billions) formats large numbers automatically.
---

### 4. What is a 'Multi-row card' visual in Power BI?
A. A visual that displays multiple related card metrics grouped together in horizontal or vertical tiles
B. A table without headers
C. A scatter plot
D. An image gallery
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Multi-row cards group multiple fields into a cohesive card-like display.
---

### 5. Why are KPI cards typically placed at the very top of an executive dashboard canvas?
A. Executives read from top-to-bottom; summary health metrics belong at the top before granular details below
B. Because Power BI will not let you place them at the bottom
C. They require less power at the top
D. It improves printer speed
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Top-to-bottom visual hierarchy ensures decision-makers see primary KPIs immediately.
